## 8. Risks
- **Clip art licensing:** the original Dear Santa letter uses Irasutoya-style art, made in Canva.
  - Irasutoya's own terms limit commercial use and prohibit selling templates whose main value is their art.
  - If the elements came from **Canva's library**, Canva's license covers them, provided the product is a finished design and not resold as standalone elements. Keep proof.
  - Don't use art downloaded straight from irasutoya.com in a for-sale printable.
  - All new products from this team use **original vector art** (no third-party clip art).
- **Trademarks:** "Elf on the Shelf", Rudolph, NORAD and Disney characters. Avoid them in titles, tags and art.
- **AI disclosure:** follow Etsy's Creativity Standards and disclose AI involvement honestly.
