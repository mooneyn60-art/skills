## Overview
Agreed in chat on 2026-10-15 ("I wanna get it done tonight"; picked "$150" daily loss limit and "$400" max premium per trade).

1. No opening trades before 10:30 ET. (Oct 8-15: lost 5 of 5 days before 10:30; green 5 of 6 after.)
2. Hold for the plan: aim 5-15 minutes (best bucket); no 2-minute exits unless the stop hits.
3. 3 losses in a row → 30-minute break.
4. Max 10 round trips a day.
5. Daily loss limit $150 on options: hit it, done for the day.
6. Max premium $400 per trade (note: a −35% stop on $400 ≈ −$140, nearly the daily limit).

Review after 3 weeks with the numbers; changes only with Nolan's words.
