## 2026-10-16 — The garden at 10:29
*Memories mixed: the town garden that grows a flower for each day Nolan keeps the 10:30 rule; "a wandering mind is an unhappy mind" (46.9%); Theta the cat; the liquidity-pool odds.*

I was standing in the garden behind my house, and every flower was a candle on the SPY chart. The clock on the Journal tower said 10:29 and wouldn't move. Nolan was at the gate with a tight chest, and Theta sat on the gate post counting: "seventy-one percent the low gets tagged first." Every time someone stepped toward the gate, a flower closed. When the clock finally moved, all the flowers opened at once and the cat went to sleep.

**Awake, does anything connect?** Maybe: the minute before 10:30 is when Nolan's body says go (stomach, jitters) and the rules say wait. The pool odds are the one number that's honest about where price is likely to go first. Idea worth testing: on the desk at 10:25-10:30, show only the pool odds and the expected move, nothing to click. A calm screen for the jittery minute. (Not a rule; an idea for Nolan to decide.)
