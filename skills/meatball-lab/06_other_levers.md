Meatball Research Lab, 2026-10-27.
Ranking = expected impact × probability. Impact is measured in YPP odds, or in money that can be reinvested.

## Corrected deadline math (this changes everything else)
- Review takes about 1 month. A rejection adds a 30-day wait. Nothing says pending applications keep the old rules.
- **So the real target is: both bars met and the application filed by about Dec 15.** That leaves **7 weeks**, not 13.
- The 4,000 hours must be *qualified public long-form hours in the last 12 months*. Shorts-feed watch time does not count.

## What the channel agent is doing that works against YPP
- **Volume over variety.** 4-7 YouTube Shorts a day on one template is at the same time the review risk, the test contamination TARS flagged, and the reason the picture never changes.
- **The conspiracy pivot widened before E19 was scored.**
  - Case Shorts got **1-2 views** in their first days (TARS review), while animal v3/v4 Shorts got 950-2,000.
  - A pivot that doesn't feed watch hours isn't moving YPP.
  - **Keep cases as compilation fuel (C2) and as TikTok Story Cuts (paid at 60 s+). Don't give them 2 of 3 YouTube Short slots until E19 says so.**
  - P(cases beat animal Shorts on YouTube views by Nov 10): **25%**.
- **The north star is wrong.** Goal meters track Shorts views and subs per 1k. The binding constraint is **long-form qualified hours** (≈0 of 4,000). Put hours on the goal meter, daily, from Studio's Earn tab.

These odds are **lower than TARS's 35%** for three reasons:
1. YouTube's own 1-month review time moves the deadline to mid-December.
2. The base rate for a compilation is 100-800 views, not a breakout.
3. The channel has no long-form audience today (28 s AVD).

**The library is not wasted either way.** Every compilation keeps earning hours toward 8,000.
