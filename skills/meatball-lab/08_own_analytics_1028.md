## What this changes
1. **"No quizzes" now has evidence, on the right metric.**
   - The Mayor's HW4 found quiz and non-quiz *views* equal (1,211 vs 1,188), but views mix reach and timing.
   - On **stayed-to-watch** the gap is huge: TRUE-or-FALSE openers at 20-22% vs house-series and story openers at 45-59%. **Nolan's rule holds.** Keep it, and log the evidence in the experiment row.
   - Correction to my HW4 grade: the rule was taste *on views*, but it's supported *on retention*.
2. **The drawn "Things Living In Your House" series has the best openers on the channel** (58.6% and 44.9%). It's early (n=2), but it fits everything else we found: an "about you" hook, the thing shown at frame 0, a drawn world.
   - P(the series' stayed-to-watch median is ≥45% over its first 6 parts): **65%**.
3. **Long-form viewers leave in the first minute (11-16% viewed of 7-10 min).**
   - My compilation maths used 4-8 min average view for a 45-60 min list. At 11-16%, a 45 min list gives **5-7 min**, inside that range, if a compilation holds like a 7-10 min episode. **It might not**, so measure C1 at 7 days.
   - The fix for the first minute is in the opening: no intro, the strangest item first, chapters visible.
4. **2.5% returning viewers is the real sub-conversion problem.** People enjoy single clips but never meet *Meatball*. Visible series numbering ("#3 of Things Living In Your House"), Related video to the compilation, and the same host and banner on every Short are the levers.
   - P(returning share rises to ≥4% within 30 days of consistent numbered series): **45%**.
5. **The 39.5% channel stayed-to-watch is the gate for everything.** Every point gained there multiplies reach.
   - The quiz and photo-slideshow back catalogue drags the average down.
   - **New uploads should only be the 45%+ formats** (house series, story openers). Pause anything that opens on a question card.

## Addendum: the full YouTube Studio AI report (Nolan's screen recording, 10/28 3:17 pm)
**Retention curves**
- **Daddy Longlegs (best):**
  - 0:00-0:04 stays above 120% (viewers rewind to look at the creepy visuals).
  - 0:05-0:15 drops from ~114% to ~69% as viewers absorb the answer.
  - Flattens around 54% to the end, with no exit spike.
- **Porcupines TRUE/FALSE (worst):**
  - Steady at ~106% for 0:00-0:04.
  - **0:04-0:08 is the big drop, 106% to 75%, exactly when the narration leaves the trivia setup.**
  - Then a steady slide to 15.7% by the end.
- **Lesson:** the leak is the moment the hook hands over to the explanation. Shorten that handover, and keep the creepy picture on screen while the answer starts.

**Subscribers and returning viewers (this week)**
