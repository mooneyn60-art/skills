## Bottom line
1. **On YouTube Shorts, stick-figure explainers have a low ceiling right now, and Meatball is already at it.**
   - 53 recent Shorts from the two real stick-figure explainer channels I could find on YouTube have a **median of 846 views and a max of 3,310**. 26% got under 100 views.
   - The 50-channel peer set vidIQ returned for "stick figure animated explainers" is mostly tiny "Educational Animation" channels at about **160-1,000 average views** and about 50 subs.
   - Meatball averages **~480 views/video, with animal Shorts at 950-2,000, on 959 subs**. That is top-quartile for its own cohort.
   - **The format is not underperforming on YouTube; the format has a ceiling there.**
2. **The same stick-figure explainers break out on TikTok and Instagram, not YouTube.**
   - Odds of Mind's stick-figure explainer got **6.5M views on Instagram** (9.6x its median). Its YouTube Shorts in the same style have a median of **846**.
   - Three of the biggest stick-figure explainer creators on IG/TikTok (whitepageofficial 1.9M, historicaldoodle 1.6M, lianna_anima 3M) **have no YouTube channel under those handles**.
   - everythingprofessor (1.8M on IG) has **zero** YouTube Shorts.
   - The best creators in our exact style don't bother with YouTube Shorts. (Inference; I can't see their reasons.)
3. **Stick-figure breakouts run long, not ≤20 s.**
   - TikTok stick-figure outliers with known length: **n=12, median 125 s, range 56-313 s, none ≤20 s**.
   - On YouTube, within the two stick-figure channels: ≤20 s median **138 views** (n=6), 21-40 s **807** (n=30), >40 s **1,116** (n=17).
   - The ≤20 s group is mostly Dr. Stickman's comedy skits, so content is confounded with length. But nothing here supports ≤20 s for stick-figure narration.
4. **Pivoting a stick-figure fact channel to a different genre killed it.**
   - Dr. Stickman Explains moved from body-fact Shorts (median **1,145**, n=21) to stickman comedy skits (median **42**, n=9; 67% under 100 views).
   - **This is a direct warning for the Meatball Investigates pivot.** Our animal Shorts got 950-2,000 views; the first case Shorts got 1-2 views.

## The Factello natural experiment (3D body explainers, not stick, but the only clean length test found)
Factello switched from 21 s to 11 s Shorts (Sept 20-Oct 20) and then back to 21 s, with the same template, topic and character.
- **11 s: median 4,442 (n=28)**
- **21 s: median 3,388 (n=21)**

That is +31% for 11 s, but it's a before/after, so channel growth over time is confounded with length. It is the only real data point for "shorter wins", it is not a stick-figure channel, and the effect is small. **Verdict: length is a 1.3x lever at most. Format and platform are 10-1,000x levers.**

## What this means (blunt)
- **Keep the stick figure.** It is not why views are low. Stick-figure explainers get 1-6.5M where the platform rewards them.
  - The NICHE_PLAYBOOK point still holds: draw the *world* in the same doodle style as Meatball. That is cheaper for a stick figure than for a full character kit (item 05: skip the $250-600 host kit and spend on doodle backgrounds and animals).
- **YouTube Shorts in this format will not deliver 10M views in 90 days.** P(stick-figure YouTube Shorts alone reach 10M/90 days before Feb 1): **<2%**.
- **TikTok is where the format pays, and the channel is already Creator Rewards-eligible there (60 s+).** The stick-figure outliers sit at 56-313 s, which lines up with Creator Rewards.
- **YouTube's job for a stick-figure channel is long-form watch hours** (item 01), not Shorts virality. Stick-figure long-form compilations do work on YouTube: the 5-hour Sam O'Nella compilation got 115K views on a 7.4K-sub channel.

## Recommendations (each with my probability)
1. **Launch "Why It Sucks to Be Born as a [Animal]" as the main TikTok series, 60-120 s, with Meatball as the animal-stand-in.** Cut a 30-45 s YouTube Short from each.
   - P(it beats our TikTok median of 197 views by 3x within 10 posts): **55%**.
   - P(one post passes 100K within 20 posts): **25%**.
2. **Second series: "What It Feels Like to Be Eaten by [X]"**, second person, Meatball inside the animal.
   - P(beats the TikTok median by 3x within 10 posts): **50%**.
3. **Stop shortening stick-figure Shorts toward 20 s on YouTube. Test 30-45 s instead**, the band where the stick-figure channels sit and where we can fit a story.
   - P(30-45 s beats ≤20 s on views per Short for our stick-figure animal Shorts): **55%**. The market data leans this way, but our n=1 (Daddy Longlegs) leans the other.
4. **Don't let the case-file pivot take over the YouTube Shorts slots.** Dr. Stickman's pivot from facts to skits cut its median 27x, and our first case Shorts are at 1-2 views. Keep animal facts as the YouTube Shorts core.
   - P(cases beat animal Shorts on YouTube by Nov 10): **25%** (unchanged).
5. **Reweight the YPP plan toward long-form compilations of stick-figure animal content** ("Why It Sucks to Be Born as… The Complete Collection", 45-60 min; "Weird Animal Facts to Fall Asleep To").
   - P(YouTube Shorts contribute more to YPP than compilations do by Jan 31): **20%**.

## Caveats
- Only 2 real stick-figure explainer channels on YouTube were found and measured (53 Shorts). vidIQ's YouTube semantic search mostly returns stickman *meme* animations (Alan Becker-style edits) for "stick figure". That itself suggests few stick-figure *explainer* channels exist on YouTube Shorts.
- The peer set (50 channels) is vidIQ's similarity match. I didn't verify every channel's art style, and many may be doodle-style rather than strict stick figures.
- The "these creators aren't on YouTube" point is based on handle lookups. Some may use different YouTube handles.
- The TikTok/IG data covers outliers only (winners). It shows what winners look like, not the odds of winning.
