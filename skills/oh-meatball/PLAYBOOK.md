# Oh Meatball playbook (only lessons backed by our own numbers or research/10-11)

## Proven
- Audio: every post goes out with voices + theme music + sting, -14 LUFS (finish.py QA). Silent posts flopped.
- Never post a part twice on one platform: duplicates split views.

- Loudness: upload the finish.py master via opusclip_create_upload_link (OpusClip render -16 LUFS) instead of
  the vidiq_compose copy (-20 LUFS). No compose needed; saves credits. (Pt 3 vs Pt 1-2, 2026-10-15)
- Duplicates: set extra copies to Private, never delete-and-reupload (YouTube treats that as spam).
