# Oh Meatball playbook (only lessons backed by our own numbers or research/10-11)

## Proven
- Audio: every post goes out with voices + theme music + sting, -14 LUFS (finish.py QA). Silent posts flopped.
- Never post a part twice on one platform: duplicates split views.

- Loudness: upload the finish.py master via opusclip_create_upload_link (OpusClip render -16 LUFS) instead of
  the vidiq_compose copy (-20 LUFS). No compose needed; saves credits. (Pt 3 vs Pt 1-2, 2026-10-15)
- Duplicates: set extra copies to Private, never delete-and-reupload (YouTube treats that as spam).

## From research (not yet proven on our channel): see SKILL.md "Breakout rules"
- **Shorts views give 0 watch hours** (Shorts feed hours don't count; Shorts and long-form are recommended separately, per Metricool 2026). A linked Short asks for the tap in its last 3-5 s, and the long-form pays off that promise in its first 10 s (research/60 R60-1/2; E29).
- **Test & Compare every long-form** with 3 titles or pairs. The winner is judged by watch time per impression, not CTR (R60-4).
- **Auto dubbing on** (no YPP needed per the Help page; "too fast" speech is ineligible, so long-form narration may need +5%) (R60-3).
- **TikTok volume raises the ceiling, not the median** (Buffer, 11.4M posts). Keep 3 a day and score weeks by p90 and 2x-median hits (R60-5).
- **TikTok search triple:** the search phrase is spoken in line 1, shown in the title bar, and in the first 50 characters of the caption. Topics come from Creator Search Insights → Content gap, weekly (R60-6/7).
- **Collabs tag, not shout-outs:** a co-authored video is recommended to both channels' audiences (R60-9). Hype is post-YPP only (R60-10).
