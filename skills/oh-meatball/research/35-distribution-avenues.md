  - in-app TikTok sounds are licensed for TikTok only, not for the YouTube/IG copies;
  - personal-account "trending sounds" aren't commercially cleared.
- **Formats to copy this week** (all [M]):
  1. **"Part 1 | [corrected belief]" 2-3 min stick-figure explainer** (TikTok, IG). Topic: *"Your pet doesn't see you as a human / Do animals know they're pets?"* It's hot on TikTok (2.1M) and YouTube search (+732%) at once. We'd add our own angle (myths about what pets think), not copy theirs.
  2. **Expectation vs reality**, e.g. "Most cobras: [hood] / actually: [calm]": a 2-photo, 15 s Short.
  3. **"Don't make the mistake of…" safety myth** (camel water, 1M on 1.7K followers). Fits our danger-myth winners (research/28, 34).
  4. **Debunk a viral fake** ("Giraffes in trucks" debunk: 2.3M on 888 followers). It fits the trend playbook (research/33 §4), but **never show the fake clip**: describe it, then show CC photos.
  5. **Binary-sort game** ("Friendly or not?", "Can you pet it? Yes/No", 1M+ each). Our TRUE/FALSE as a rapid 6-animal round, scored "how many did you get?". It's a comment trigger.
