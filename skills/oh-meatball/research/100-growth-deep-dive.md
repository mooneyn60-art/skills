## Stop doing
- Tuning Short posting hour, weekday, gap or count for views: every test p above 0.09, rank correlations under 0.25, about 166 comparisons (shorts pull). Weekend 1,216 against 1,024 is a lead on 17 Shorts, not a reason to move slots.
- Title tricks for views inside one topic: number in the title (1,155 against 1,185 in drawn v6, p 0.89), title length, emoji (65 of 66 have one). Research 93 found the same.
- Work on cut rate, first-word time or pace of the opening (research 96: already where the winners are).
- Reading average % viewed across Shorts of different lengths: 14 to 16 s Shorts loop above 100% (rho -0.68 with length).
- Treating "longer Shorts earn more subscribers" as a lead: it does not hold (see below).
- Building Shorts for the slow search tail: after day 2 a Short earns about 1% of its views (below). Search is about 140 views a day in total.

## What failed the check (recomputed with pandas and scipy, about 15 comparisons of my own)
1. **Decay.** Claim: day 0 72%, after day 2 3.0%. The 19 Shorts kept were those with a Windsor row on every day 0 to 5; a day with 0 views has no row, so the filter drops the Shorts that died fastest and lifts the late share. On all 39 Shorts with 300+ views and data to day 5 (`90-youtube-video-day.csv`): **day 0 85%, day 1 13%, day 2 2.5%, after day 2 1.1% (middle half 0.5 to 2.8%)**. Research 90's 90% is closer.
2. **The top-ten subscriber Shorts.** 11 Shorts are tied at 2 subscribers, so "9 of 10 house bug or spider" and "6 of 10 are the 14 to 16 s cut" depend on which ties were picked. Fair version: of the 17 Shorts with 2 or more subscribers, 13 are house bug or spider (76%), against 40 of 66 Shorts overall (61%).
3. **"Two of the top three are the longer cuts" as a lead.** Length does not go with subscribers: rank r -0.11 with subscribers (n=54, p 0.41), -0.03 with subscribers per 1,000 views (p 0.85). Per 1,000 views: 16 s or less 1.00 (16 Shorts), 17 to 21 s 0.64 (10), over 21 s 1.00 (28).
4. **Spider search share p 0.0014.** A rank test gives p 0.011. Either way it is above the pull's own 0.001 bar, and 8 of the 12 spider Shorts were posted 10/24 to 10/30, so they have had longer to collect search. A lead; and no new spider Shorts are being built.
5. **Minor.** Engaged share for "other animals" is 0.35 on all set-A Shorts (0.33 with the 100-view filter). Long-video minutes are 623 in the daily table and 607 when summed by traffic source (rounding per source); hours 10.4 against 10.1.
