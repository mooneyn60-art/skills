## What it means
1. The animal question ("bear vs lion who would win") has its own search, all year, and fits "Would Meatball Survive?".
2. The team phrase spikes on game week but its results are football; an animal video there is the odd one out. It could be clicked for the joke or skipped. Not measurable ahead of time, and TikTok search demand cannot be read from here at all.
3. So the video should be built to win the animal search and use the game as the hook and the timing. Then it does not die when the game ends.
