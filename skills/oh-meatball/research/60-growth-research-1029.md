# 60 · Growth research: faster to YPP, TikTok breakout (2026-10-29)

Only what research/17, 26-29, 42, 47, 51, 56-59 don't already cover. 24 web calls, no vidIQ, no Metricool.
"Vendor" = a tool seller's blog or sample, so treat the numbers as directional. "Unverified" = no primary source found.

## Findings

### YouTube: Shorts → watch hours
- **Shorts views don't lift long-form ranking on their own.** Metricool's 2026 study (799,718 videos, 71,177 accounts): Shorts feed = 61% of views; long-form views +76% YoY but AVD -37% (~2.5 min); Shorts AVD ~16 s. Metricool says the Shorts and long-form recommenders run separately. (vendor) https://metricool.com/press-release-youtube-study-2026/ · https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/08/03/youtube-shorts-long-form-study-metricool/
  - So our 22k+ Shorts views add ~0 watch hours unless a viewer taps through. The Related video link is the only bridge (research/56 lever 1), and it has to be *asked for*.
- **YouTube's own Related video advice (blog.youtube, 2026-07-14):** spoken CTA in the Short's **last 5 s** telling people to tap the link, plus a visual cue (point at the link / flash the long-form thumbnail); link to what the viewer wants next; the long-form's **first 5-10 s must pay off the Short's promise**, no logo/intro. https://blog.youtube/creator-and-artist-stories/youtube-related-videos-traffic-guide/
- **Long-form frequency:** Metricool finds 2-4 long-forms/week is the sweet spot for long-form views (vendor). Our cap (2/week) sits at the low end; no evidence that more helps before retention is fixed. Same tubefilter link.
- **Auto dubbing is on by default for eligible channels; the Help page lists no YPP requirement.** English dubs into 20 languages (Hindi, Punjabi, Spanish, Portuguese, Indonesian, Arabic, Bengali, French, German …). Ineligible: no or little speech, music-only, **speech too fast to dub**, copyrighted content, >120 min. "Publish manually" lets you review dubs first. https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15569972 · rollout to all creators Feb 2026, 6M+ daily viewers watch 10+ min of dubbed content: https://ppc.land/youtube-auto-dubbing-tops-6m-daily-viewers-expands-to-27-languages
  - Dub tracks play on the same video, so their watch time should count toward the 4,000 h (inference, unverified). No source shows a watch-time lift. Our narration runs at +15% rate, which may trip "too fast".
- **Test & Compare tests titles too (since Dec 2025):** up to 3 titles, thumbnails, or pairs per long-form, up to 2 weeks; the winner is the highest **watch time per impression**, not CTR. https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-title-a-b-testing-rolls-out-globally-to-creators · https://ppc.land/youtube-expands-a-b-testing-to-include-titles-alongside-thumbnails/
- **Studio "Intro" = % still watching at 30 s.** vidIQ's heuristic is 60%+, with 65%+ for strong hooks (vendor; matches our existing 60% gate). https://vidiq.com/blog/post/increase-audience-retention-youtube/ · https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1715160
- **Hype is a post-YPP lever:** long-form only, uploaded in the last 7 days, channels with 500-500K subs **in YPP**; each viewer gets 3 hypes a week; smaller channels earn more points per hype. (third-party citing the Help Center; official page 404'd) https://www.makeinfluence.com/en/academy/youtube-hype-the-fan-powered-discovery-feature-for-emerging-creators
- **Collabs tag:** a co-creator accepts an invite, both avatars show under the title, and the video is recommended to **both** audiences; the uploader keeps the revenue. The cap is reported at 5-10. (press, not the Help page) https://www.emarketer.com/content/youtube-collab-feature-reshape-influencer-strategy · https://influencermarketinghub.com/youtube-co-authoring-clips/
- **Breakout patterns in our niche:** I found no sourced 2026 case study for "would you survive" or tier-list animal channels. One vendor audit lists the "Why It Sucks To Be Born As…" animated format among the top animal-facts benchmarks (~582K views, undated) and flags deep-sea and extinct animals as gaps. (vendor, unverified) https://blog.autonolab.com/niches/2026-01-20-faceless-youtube-animal-facts/

### TikTok
- **Posting frequency:** Buffer, 11.4M posts / 150K accounts, fixed-effects (Oct 2025), against a 1 post/week baseline: +17% views/post at 2-5 a week, +29% at 6-10, +34% at 11+. The **median stays ~460-506** at every frequency; the **90th percentile climbs** (3.7K → 14.4K). More posts raise the ceiling, not the floor. No data on spacing. https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-should-you-post-on-tiktok/
  - We post 21 a week (the 11+ bucket). Judge posts by the p90 and the breakouts, not by a falling median.
- **For You signals (TikTok newsroom):** finishing a longer video is a strong signal. Captions, sounds and hashtags are inputs. **Follower count and past hits are not direct factors.** The feed usually avoids two videos in a row from the same creator, so spacing posts hours apart is safe. https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you/
- **Search indexing:** vendors agree that speech transcripts, on-screen text (OCR) and the caption are all indexed, with the keyword early in each. They disagree on how much of the caption carries weight (first 50, 100 or 150 chars). TikTok publishes no ranking weights. (vendor, unverified) https://wetheflywheel.com/en/ai-search/how-to-rank-in-tiktok-search/ · https://ttcalculator.net/guides/growth/tiktok-seo-search-ranking/
- **Creator Search Insights:** reach it by searching "creator search insights" in the app. Its **Content gap** tab lists topics searched often but covered by few videos. "Searches by followers" needs 1K+ followers. The tool also gives per-post search views, search impressions, CTR and rank. https://buffer.com/resources/creator-search-insights/ · https://metricool.com/tiktok-creator-insights/
  - "Search value" is one of the 4 named Rewards factors (research/57), so Content gap topics are the cheapest RPM lever.
- **Creator Rewards:** nothing new from TikTok. Its help page is JS-only and wouldn't render. Third-party sources: still $0.40-1.00 RPM, 60 s+ videos, and they disagree on whether search views qualify (research/57 assumed For You only). https://stackinfluence.com/blog/tiktok-pay-per-view-what-creators-earn-in-2026 · https://www.argil.ai/blog/does-tiktok-pay-for-views
- **Playlists:** reported for ~10K+ followers (we're at ~9.4K). The 2021 rules: name ≤15 characters, public videos only, **one playlist per video**. (unverified for 2026) https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/tiktok-launches-new-playlists-feature-to-group-clips-into-themed-collecti/597460 · https://publer.com/blog/tiktok-playlists/

### Community / returning viewers
- I found no primary source measuring the lift from replies or pins. The levers that are documented are structural: the Related video link, Collabs (shown to both audiences) and, after YPP, Hype (fans push a new long-form for 7 days). Figures like the end-screen playlist "+3.2 min/session" are unverified vendor numbers. https://www.nexlev.io/youtube-end-screen-tips

## RULES TO ADOPT
1. **R60-1 Ask for the tap.** A Short whose Related video points at a *public* long-form ends on a spoken "the full story's in the link below", with Meatball pointing down at the link, in the last 3-5 s. On those Shorts this replaces the subscribe pill. Log it as E29 (Related-video clicks and long-form views from Shorts, vs Shorts without the line; 7 days).
2. **R60-2 The long-form pays off the Short in 10 s.** Each long-form's first 10 s shows the animal or claim its linked Shorts promise. Shorts link only to the long-form that answers them (data/related-video-map.csv).
3. **R60-3 Turn on auto dubbing (Nolan, 2 min):** Studio → Settings → Upload defaults/Advanced → Automatic dubbing on, "Publish manually". Review the Hindi, Spanish and Portuguese dubs on the next long-form. If Studio says the speech is too fast, render long-form narration at +5% instead of +15%. Read dub-track watch time in Studio after 14 days.
4. **R60-4 Every long-form runs Test & Compare with 3 title variants** (or 3 title+thumb pairs). Read the winner by watch-time share, not CTR. Nolan starts it in Studio at publish.
5. **R60-5 TikTok: keep 3 a day; judge by breakouts.** Score a week by its 90th-percentile organic views and the count of posts at 2x the median, not by the median. Cut frequency only if qualified views per post fall 25%+ over 2 weeks.
6. **R60-6 Search triple on every TikTok:** say the search phrase in the first spoken line, show it in the title bar, and put it in the first 50 characters of the caption.
7. **R60-7 Weekly Content-gap pull (Nolan, Monday, 2 min):** screenshot Creator Search Insights → Suggested → Content gap (Science/Animals) to the town square. The best animal topic on it becomes that week's list video (E26) and a YouTube search title.
8. **R60-8 TikTok playlists at 10K:** the day the option appears, make "In Your House" and "Survive?" (≤15 chars, one playlist per video), then add new posts in series order.
9. **R60-9 Collabs, not shout-outs:** re-targeted collab drafts (research/56 lever 9) ask the partner to accept a YouTube Collabs tag on one original video each.
10. **R60-10 Post-YPP (park until accepted):** ask viewers to Hype each new long-form in its first 7 days.
