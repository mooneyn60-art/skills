# 18 — Thumbnail teardown of the niche's breakouts (2026-10-22)

Looked at the actual thumbnails of the top outliers in `16-myths-niche-outliers.md`.

## What the winners share
- **Clean light background** for the cartoon/mascot channels: white or off-white paper (Kelevin "complete lies", What Gives? "Weird ANIMAL FACTS #6", "ANIMAL ~~FACTS~~ lies #2"). No photo backgrounds, no sunbursts.
- **Strike-through headline**: "ANIMAL ~~FACTS~~ lies" (What Gives?, Part 2 scored 8.2x). The red scribble is the hook.
- **Several animals at once**, each clearly separated, which signals "list video". Lying Pie and Oddly Factual (46-54x) put a red ✗ or a "MYTH" stamp on each animal.
- **A stick-figure/cartoon face with an exaggerated reaction** (Kelevin, What Gives?).
- **Series numbering** "#2", "#6" in the corner.
- Monster/cryptid winners use dramatic close-ups plus "EXTINCT → ALIVE" or "this is REAL" with an arrow.

## What ours did wrong (v1, v2)
- v1: dark full-frame photo, small text: a brown blob at feed size.
- v2: sunburst + framed photo read as a kids' channel, and one animal didn't say "list".
