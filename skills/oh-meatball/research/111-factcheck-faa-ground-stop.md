## Sources opened
- CBS News (updated 6:52 PM ET): https://www.cbsnews.com/news/faa-issues-nationwide-ground-stop/ . FAA: "extraordinary circumstances and for reasons of safety". Duffy: "issued a nationwide ground stop for all U.S. airports". The network update, reboot and crash are CBS's own sourced reporting, paraphrased; the system is run by L3Harris, which did not immediately comment. The FAA and the White House called it a "telecommunication outage". "Nefarious": the headline says White House, but the speaker is Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin at a briefing outside the White House: "there was no indication, as of yet, that this was a nefarious act against us." About 3,100 airborne aircraft were bound for U.S. airports (Flightradar24); Mullin said airborne flights were handled "case-by-case".
- NBC News live blog (6:44 PM ET entry): https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/live-blog/live-updates-flights-grounded-faa-communications-outage-rcna609268 . Duffy announced at 4:16 p.m. on X: "communication issue impacting all Air Route Traffic Control Centers". FAA: departing flights grounded "due to extraordinary circumstances and for reasons of safety". **Microsoft: three unnamed senior officials told NBC News nothing indicates a connection.** International flights "case by case"; SFO Pacific-bound flights cleared at 3 p.m. local.
- ABC News (5:44 PM): https://abcnews.com/US/nationwide-ground-stop-ordered-airports-faa/story?id=138121848 . Mullin: "It's just a telecommunications outage as of now." "No additional aircraft are taking off at this time. Flights that are currently in the air will be diverted to other airports." No Microsoft link.
- Scripps News: https://www.scrippsnews.com/us-news/faa-issues-nationwide-ground-stop-on-us-air-traffic . Duffy: "...following a communication issue impacting all Air Route Traffic Control Centers"; airborne flights allowed to continue and kept landing. No number of centers given (the pack's "22" is not confirmed and is not in the script).
- CNBC: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/faa-ground-stop.html . Start about 4 p.m. ET; FAA: "temporary telecommunications outage"; airlines said airborne flights would keep operating; about 600 flights diverted; cause "wasn't immediately clear".
- News On 6: https://www.newson6.com/tulsa-oklahoma-news/faa-reportedly-issues-nationwide-ground-stop (advisory start 2:54 p.m. CT, until further notice).

Main findings: (1) the stop is on departures, international flights are case by case and some have left, so "every flight in America just got grounded" overstates it; (2) "nefarious act" was said by the Homeland Security Secretary, not "the White House"; (3) the "not related to Microsoft" line comes from senior officials speaking to NBC (not Mullin, as the pack had it); (4) airborne flights mostly kept flying, but some were diverted (ABC, CNBC); (5) the CBS network-update report is still CBS's sourced reporting only, neither confirmed nor denied.
