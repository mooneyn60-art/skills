## Mistakes and fixes
1. **Text-only first frame on Shorts.**
   - Mistake: 60% swiped while looking at a white card with words and no animal.
   - Fix (done 10/24): the Shorts quiz card shows the animal photo from frame 1, with the claim above it and the countdown on the photo.
2. **Shorts too long.**
   - Mistake: they ran 27-88 s, and the 88 s one got the fewest views. Outlier single-myth Shorts run 17-30 s (research/23).
   - Fix (done): ~18 s cut with a seamless loop.
3. **Titles didn't match search.**
   - Mistake: titles like "TRUE or FALSE: X? 🤔 #shorts" and "Disney Lied To You About...". Search is our 2nd-biggest source.
   - Fix (done): every Short is titled with the question people type, e.g. "Can Porcupines Really Shoot Their Quills? 🦔". The text is in `short_title` per quiz beat.
4. **Long-form has no on-ramp.**
   - Mistake: zero suggested traffic and zero views.
   - Fix (Nolan, in YouTube Studio): set each Short's "Related video" to that day's episode. This is the only link from a Short into a long video; the API can't set it.
   - Until long-form gets traffic, judge it on search: give episodes titles that answer a searched question.
5. **No reason to subscribe.**
   - Mistake: 0 subscribers from 5.6k views. Nothing recurring tells a viewer there is more of this.
   - Fix (next): numbered series ("Animal Lie #14") and the YOU vs MEATBALL running score (research/22).
6. **Engagement is near zero.**
   - Mistake: about 1 comment per Short (ours) and 1-2% likes. The quiz never asks for an answer on screen.
   - Fix (next): pinned comment phrased as a poll, plus the TRUE/FALSE quiz sticker in the Shorts app (Nolan).
7. **Changing the style every few hours.**
   - Mistake: changing several things at once means we can't tell what worked.
   - Rule from now: one change per week per format, logged in data/experiments.csv with stayed-to-watch before and after.
