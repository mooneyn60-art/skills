## What to copy for Meatball
- **Size:** the character takes 40-70% of the frame height in medium shots. Close-ups (a panicked face, a chest) fill the frame. Characters only shrink to 10-20% in crowd or scale gags. The lower third stays clear for captions, which never cover the face.
- **Limbs:** rigid straight segments rotating at real joints (shoulder, elbow, hip, knee). Bends are sharp angles, not rubber-hose curves. Stroke is bold and uniform (about 6-8 px at 1080 wide) with rounded caps.
- **Timing:** pose to pose. Hit a key pose and hold it 1-2 s with a tiny idle drift, then ease in and out over a few frames to the next one. Keep it to about 2-4 key changes a second at most across the whole scene. Motion renders at 24-30 fps.
- **Face:** simple dots or ovals for eyes and a single stroke for the mouth. Slanted eyebrow dashes sell worry or anger. Special eyes: X (dead/defeated), closed arcs (bliss). Expressions SNAP between a few clean drawings; they don't morph or flicker.
- **Idle:** a breathing bob of 1-3 px, or a still hold. No big bounce.
- **Emotion glow:** a soft colored halo behind the character: yellow = warning, red = pain or danger, green = good or healing. Normal state is a soft white rim so he pops off the background.
- **Accents:** a scale-pop for sudden realization; a clear crouch before a jump; an instant squat on landing. Otherwise little squash and stretch; the movement is functional and snappy.
- **Walk:** a simple scissor stride, with a small bob at the hips when the legs pass each other and opposite arms swinging.
- **Props:** separate layers drawn in front of the hand; when grabbed, a prop moves with the hand.
- **Backgrounds:** desaturated or soft so the character pops. For us, the animal photo goes slightly dimmed or blurred behind Meatball in HOST shots and stays sharp in SHOW shots.
- **Camera:** static shots, a hard cut every 1.5-2.5 s, plus slow push-ins on key moments and occasional pans.
- **Captions:** bold white/yellow text, word by word, centered in the lower third.
