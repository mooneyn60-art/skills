# 36 — Partnerships pipeline: collabs, sponsors, zoos, licensing (Partnerships Manager, 2026-10-24)

**Short answer.** At our size, collabs are the cheapest growth lever and sponsors are a TikTok-first, $50-250 market.
Paid zoo deals don't exist. Zoos and aquariums run *unpaid* creator programs, and their value for us is expert
fact-checks and credibility, not money. Self-serve affiliates (Bookshop.org, KiwiCo, MEL Science, Chewy) are open now
but earn about $0 until a long-form episode breaks out. Licensing to edu platforms waits until we have a catalog and
YPP, and our AI voice is a real obstacle there (§6).

Builds on research/27 (fast monetization), 29 (distribution funnel), 32 (revenue streams). Does not repeat them.

### Collab mechanics and rules
- **YouTube Collaborations [OFF]:** invite **up to 5 creators** as collaborators on a long-form video or a Short. It
  works on archived live streams, not active ones. Viewers see it on desktop, mobile and TV. In Studio it's under the
  video's Details > Show more > Audience > "Collaboration" ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16554898)).
  The collaborator accepts from their own channel. Eligibility rules are not on the excerpt we could read [UNV], so
  Nolan does a test invite on one video first. vidIQ's blog says the limit is 10 and that the uploader keeps all revenue
  ([vidIQ, Jul 2026](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-collabs/), [3P]; it conflicts with Help, so trust Help).
  Some guides say tagged videos get recommended to every collaborator's subscribers
  ([AIR Media-Tech](https://air.io/en/youtube-hacks/youtube-collaboration-types-2026), [3P, UNV]).
  **This is Studio-only for us (Nolan), like Related video.**
- **Shorts remix [OFF]:** our Shorts and long-form are **remixable by default**. Remix types are Sound, Cut, Collab
  (side-by-side) and Green Screen. Non-partners can switch remixing off per video in Studio. We get a daily summary
  notification when someone remixes us ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10623810)). **Keep it
  on**: every remix of a Meatball Short credits us with a link back. The Shorts Collab tool dates from 2024 and works
  with videos up to 60 s ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=2576746), [3P]).
  - research/29 found that "Edit into a Short" from our own long-form is the remix that helps the funnel most. Keep
    doing that; remixing *other* channels is for collabs only.
- **TikTok:** Duets and Stitches **earn $0 Creator Rewards**, and so does sponsored content (research/32). A TikTok
  collab therefore means each creator posts an original 60-s+ video and tags the other in the caption. Don't duet.
- **Credit and originality:** a myth swap uses our own animation and script about their myth, never a re-upload of their
  footage. That keeps both channels clear of reused-content flags (research/27, 30).

**Monthly (first Friday):** re-check the terms that drift (KiwiCo, Chewy, TikTok One floor, YouTube Collaborations

**Licensing our quiz Shorts to edu sites**
- **YouTube Player for Education [OFF]:** an ad-free embedded player in LMSs (Canvas, Moodle). Creators are paid from
  edu partners' licensing fees by share of watch time. You sign payment terms in Studio > Earn > "More ways to earn" when
  YouTube offers a contract, sent monthly to newly eligible creators
  ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15503014), [YouTube blog](https://blog.youtube/inside-youtube/youtube-player-education-classroom-learning/)).
  Eligibility is not spelled out; we assume YPP is required [UNV]. **Keep embedding on** so we're eligible the day we
  qualify. This is the cheapest licensing path: no sales work.
- **Boclips:** licenses about 2.2M videos from 550+ brands (TED, PBS, FT) to schools and edtech, per video or by
  subscription ([case study](https://productforlearning.substack.com/p/case-study-boclips-route-to-market),
  [Vendr](https://www.vendr.com/marketplace/boclips), [3P]). It works with established brands; revisit with 100+ episodes.
- **Obstacle:** edu buyers are moving against AI content (TPT's AI ban, research/32). Our AI voice is the main reason a
  licensing deal could fail. If licensing ever matters, a human-voiced "classroom cut" is the fix [I].
