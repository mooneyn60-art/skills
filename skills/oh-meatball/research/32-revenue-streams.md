## 2. TikTok Creator Rewards: how to earn the most
**How pay works [3P, citing TikTok]:** rewards = qualified views × an RPM that TikTok sets from six factors. The
factors are originality, play duration (watch time and finish rate), search value, audience engagement, viewer location
and advertising value. TikTok publishes no rate card, and nothing shows a fixed "education" RPM. 2026 benchmarks run
$0.36-1.04 per 1k qualified views, about $0.80 on average ([Uppbeat, June 2026, via Stackinfluence](https://stackinfluence.com/blog/tiktok-pay-per-view-what-creators-earn-in-2026)).

**What a qualified view is [3P]:** a For You feed view of 5+ seconds. These don't count: paid or promoted views,
fraudulent views, views under 5 s, viewers who tapped "not interested", and integrity-flagged views. Views are unique
under TikTok's rules ([Creators Agency, verified July 2026](https://creatorsagency.co/blog/tiktok-creator-rewards-program-2026)).

**What raises RPM, in order of how much we can change it [I]:**
1. **Search value.** Put the searched question in the caption, the on-screen title and the first spoken line: "Do
   ostriches really bury their heads?", not "Myth #4". Our 12-myth quiz titles are already search phrases.
2. **Play duration.** Keep the 65-140 s range, but fill it: the reveal comes late and there are no dead seconds. A
   61-s video watched to the end beats a 140-s video abandoned at 40 s. Test 70-90 s against 120-140 s as one
   variable in experiments.csv.
3. **US audience.** Post at US times (already 10 AM / 12 PM / 6 PM ET), use US spellings and US animals (raccoons,
   possums, bald eagles). Check the audience-country split in TikTok Studio.
4. **Originality.** Each TikTok is a fresh render with its own hook and jokes, not a YouTube episode cut into pieces.
5. **Engagement.** End on the quiz question ("Comment your score") to drive comments.

**Posts per day.** TikTok publishes no limit [UNV]. 3 a day is safe and normal; there's no evidence that more raises
earnings, and research/27 shows volume without quality loses. **Hold at 3**, and add a 4th only if per-video qualified
views don't drop.

**What gets videos or the account disqualified [3P unless marked]:**
- Videos under 1 minute. Duets and Stitches. Sponsored or branded content (those videos earn $0).
- "Copied content" and "lightly modified reposts", including posting the same video twice.
- Fake views, follows or engagement. Deleting a video before its rewards settle.
- **AI:** TikTok's sister program terms say AI-generated content "is not original" and that videos "produced primarily by
  artificial intelligence will not count" ([Creator Growth Challenge terms, OFF](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/global/tiktok-creator-follower-challenge-program-terms-conditions/en)).
  The Creator Rewards terms reportedly require content "produced entirely by the creator and/or adds new ideas"
  ([Storrito summary, UNV](https://storrito.com/resources/what-tiktoks-ai-monetization-restrictions-signal-for-creator-income/)).
  - Our defence: our own character, our own animation code, written jokes, and editorial choices about which myths to
    use. **Keep the AI label on** (we use a realistic AI voice).
  - Never ship a video that is only TTS over a photo slideshow. That is the pattern automated originality checks flag,
    and they also catch some original creators by mistake in 2026 [3P].

## 3. Facebook and Instagram: the Fast Track trap
- **Fast Track is the best new money we can get, and it is easy to lose.** You are eligible only if you "have not posted
  a Facebook reel in the past 6 months" ([OFF](https://creators.facebook.com/creator-fast-track)). Three ways we could
  break that by accident:
  1. Connecting Facebook in Metricool and crossposting.
  2. Instagram's "share to Facebook" toggle.
  3. Nolan testing one reel by hand.
- Reels already posted on TikTok or YouTube count as eligible, as long as they "hasn't already been posted on Facebook"
  [OFF]. So our back catalog of masters supplies the 15 reels a month.
- Pay tiers: $100-450/mo at 20k-99k followers, $1,000 at 100k+, $3,000 at 1M+, for 3 months. Meta "reserves the right to
  determine eligibility" [OFF]. A [Meta announcement, March 2026](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/03/creator-fast-track-grow-your-audience-earn-money-on-facebook/).
- **Metricool crossposting:** the API-posted Reel is our own master with no watermark, so it should count [I, UNV]. Meta's
  page does not mention third-party tools. Watch the first month's Fast Track dashboard to confirm.
- **Content Monetization** (In-stream + Reels + Performance bonus, merged) is still **invite-only** in October 2026, with no
  published threshold ([Neal Schaffer, Sep 2026](https://nealschaffer.com/facebook-content-monetization/),
  [creators.facebook.com](https://creators.facebook.com/tools/facebook-content-monetization)). Fast Track gives
  "immediate access" to it [OFF]. Third-party "10k followers / 600k minutes" figures are old-program leftovers [3P].
- **Instagram** pays no Reels bonuses we can count on (invite-only, time-limited). Gifts start at about 500 followers,
  where 1 star = $0.01. Subscriptions start at about 10k ([IMH](https://influencermarketinghub.com/instagram-subscriptions-gifts/), [3P]).
  Its value is reach, and its 20k followers count toward Fast Track too. Since April 2026, Instagram deprioritizes
  reposts and other apps' watermarks (research/29), so upload our masters.

  March 2026 ([Storyboard18](https://storyboard18.com/digital/youtube-opens-shopping-affiliate-program-to-creators-with-500-subscribers-93649.htm), [3P]).

  - **Amazon:** toys and pets pay about 3%, books 3-4.5% [3P, conflicting]. You need 3 sales in 180 days ([Lasso](https://getlasso.co/amazon-affiliate-commission-rate/), [3P]).

    The scope and date are unclear. Since October 1, 2026, Basic sellers pay $29 and keep 55% ([EduBracket](https://edubracket.com/articles/best-platforms-to-sell-teaching-resources-2026), [3P]).
  - Our scripts and voice are AI, so **don't list on TPT**. Sell printable packs from our own Shopify instead.

## 6. Policy traps to avoid
- **Fast Track:** one Facebook Reel, from any source, resets the 6-month clock [OFF].
- **TikTok Creator Rewards:**
  - No reposting the same video, no sub-60 s uploads in the slots meant to earn, no duets or stitches.
  - Never pay to Promote a video expecting rewards: promoted views don't qualify. No engagement pods or bought followers.
  - Don't delete videos early.
  - Keep the AI label on, and keep the work authored (written jokes, our animation), never a bare TTS slideshow.
- **TikTok LIVE:** we have no live host. Streaming a pre-recorded quiz as "LIVE" is widely reported to break LIVE
  rules [UNV]. Skip LIVE until Nolan wants to host.
- **Photo licences:** monetized posts and merch are commercial use. Use only CC0, or CC BY with attribution in the
  caption or description. **No CC BY-NC photos** in anything monetized (research/31). Merch uses Meatball art only,
  never the photos.
- **Disclosure:**
  - FTC rules require a clear, unavoidable disclosure. In short video that means spoken plus on-screen text; a tag
    hidden behind "more" doesn't count ([Kelley Drye summary](https://www.kelleydrye.com/viewpoints/blogs/ad-law-access/ftc-updates-faqs-for-endorsement-guides-offers-more-guidance-on-social-media-and-video-endorsements.md)).
  - Use "#ad" or "Paid partnership", not "#sp" or "#collab".
  - Amazon's line: "As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases." Amazon also bars its links in emails and
    PDFs [UNV, check the Operating Agreement].
- **Kids:**
  - Keep YouTube set to "not made for kids", since made-for-kids switches off comments and limits features.
  - Market to parents and teachers, never collect emails from under-13s (COPPA), and never aim affiliate pitches at children.
- **Trademarks:** no "MythBusters", no Disney or Pokémon animals, no brand logos on merch.
- **YouTube YPP review:** don't let money streams add templated volume. Repetitive uploads are the main rejection risk
  (research/26, 27).
