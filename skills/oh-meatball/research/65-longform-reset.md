## What's actually wrong

1. **YouTube is barely showing them.** 2-5 views means close to zero impressions, so this isn't a retention problem yet. Even the house long-forms got 2 and 4 views, though house is our best Shorts topic. Changing the topic didn't fix it, so the problem is **distribution**.
2. **Nothing sends viewers to them.** Shorts and long-form are recommended separately (research 61). The only official bridge is the **Related video** link on a Short, and none of our Shorts point at a long-form. Our Shorts get 1-2K views each; that traffic is going nowhere.
3. **Four formats in ten days.** Since 10/27 we've posted a conspiracy list, a house countdown, a tier list and a CIA case file, with Kill-or-Keep up next. They used two voices (eric-michael and Andrew) and three posting times (14:00, 17:00, 08:00). YouTube never sees a consistent long-form audience, and neither do viewers.
4. **The plans contradict each other.** MASTER_ORDERS says the weekly long-form is a case file (Nolan 10/30), plus a 30-60 min Sunday compilation, which has never been made. Research 64 says the next long-form is house-ranked. And the case-file lane converts worst: history/myths bring **0.2 subs per 1K views** against **1.8** for household pests, and case-file Shorts got 1-4 views.
5. **The opening loses people.** 26-44% leave in 12 s, and the Azorian cut spends ~10 s on a joke and a comment ask before the story starts (research 61).

## The reset (proposed; needs Nolan's OK where marked)

**1. One show: "Things Living In Your House", long edition.** [Nolan's call]
- Every long-form uses the same creatures and promise as the Shorts that already work (house centipede, house spiders, cockroach, bed bugs, dust mites, mice).
- Same look, same Andrew voice. The style lock is untouched.
- **Case files pause after Azorian.** Judge Azorian at 7 days. If it beats the house long-forms on views and subs, case files come back as a once-a-month special.

**2. One slot: one long-form a week, Saturday 08:00 ET.**
- Kill-or-Keep is already set for Sat 11/7 08:00, and research 64 shows weekend views peaking 08:00-13:00 ET.
- Same day, same time every week. No extra long-forms in between.
- **Drop the Sunday 30-60 min compilation** until a weekly long-form passes 500 views.

**3. One bridge: every on-topic Short points at the newest long-form.** [Studio job, Nolan only; the API can't set it]
- In Studio, set **Related video = "3 Bugs in Your House You Should Never Kill"** on the house centipede, cellar/daddy-longlegs, jumping spider, bed bug, cockroach and mouse Shorts. Those are the creatures in it.
- Pinned comment on those Shorts: "Should you kill it? Full list in the Related video 👆".
- Each Saturday, re-point the Related links to the new long-form.

**4. The packaging rules (from 61/64; already written, now enforced)**
- Title: number + creature + "your house" + enforcement ("You Should Never Kill"). Test & Compare on 3 titles every time.
- Thumbnail: one real-looking creature filling the frame, ≤3 words, readable at 168 px.
- Narration starts at 0.0 s on the creature, so the title pays off in 5 s. No joke, intro or comment ask before 0:25. A new peak every 20-30 s.

**5. Measure one thing per week (Sunday)**
- At 7 days, for each long-form: impressions, CTR, % still watching at 30 s, and views from Related video.
- The test: **does Related-video traffic show up at all?** If the Kill-or-Keep links are set and it still has <50 views at 7 days, the bridge isn't working and we change the Short CTA next.

## The honest maths
- YPP needs 4,000 watch hours; we had 63.6 on 10/28.
- At today's ~1.2 min watched per view, that's ~200K long-form views.
- Even at 1,000 views a week and 3 min watched per view, it's ~50 hours a week, so **~18 months**.
- Monetization this year depends on the long-forms reaching a regular audience, not on making more of them. That's why this reset is mostly about distribution, not production.
