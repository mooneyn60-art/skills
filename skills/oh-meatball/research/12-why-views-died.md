# 12 — Why the views died, and what changes (2026-10-16)

Sources: YouTube Analytics via vidIQ (daily views 2026-09-01..10-13, traffic sources 08-15..09-15),
vidiq_user_videos, vidIQ Shorts outliers (cat/AI story, last 3 months), research/10.

## What happened (measured)
| Period | Views/day | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| Aug 15 – Sep 13 | ~1,000–3,800 | Joe Bartolozzi clips; 93% of views from the Shorts feed, 7% search |
| Sep 14 – Oct 13 | ~40–80 | Uploads stopped Sep 12–15. Shorts views come from the feed per video and fade within days, so the channel fell off a cliff the day posting stopped |
| Oct 15 – 16 | Pt 1 = 1 view, Pt 2 = 1 view | New cat series gets almost no feed impressions |

The Bartolozzi views were not "taken away": they were tied to that content, which is now private and can't
come back (reused third-party clips do not qualify for YPP and risk claims).

## Why the cat Shorts get ~1 view (ranked, [M]=measured, [I]=inference)
1. **Wrong seed audience [I, strong].** YouTube tests a new Short on viewers like the channel's existing
   audience: Bartolozzi streamer fans. They swipe cat drama away, so the test fails and distribution stops.
2. **Duplicate uploads [M].** Part 1 went up 4 times in 20 minutes; 2 are still public. Identical re-uploads
   are a spam/repetitive signal.
3. **Fragment format [M, research/10].** 16–32 s "Part N" cliffhangers need context. Breakouts in this niche
   are 47–131 s complete mini-arcs (setup → suffering → reversal → payoff); the biggest had no part label.
4. **Wrong platform for this genre [M, research/10].** Episodic AI animal soaps break out on Instagram Reels
   and TikTok (1–39M). On YouTube Shorts no episodic AI cat soap topped ~30k; what does break out there
   is standalone 10–20 s AI cat stories (e.g. "Orange Fat Cat Funny", 1.2k subs, 675k views; "Fictional Cat
   Story", 1.5k subs, 530k views) [M, outliers 2026-10-16].
5. Possible channel-level limits (strikes, claims, "made for kids" setting) — only visible in YouTube Studio [I].

## Changes (applied from the 2026-10-17 run)
- **E5 — complete-story format.** The main daily post is ONE 45–70 s video: a 1-line "previously" from
  existing shots (free) + the 4 new shots, ending on a payoff AND a hook. No reliance on Part N context.
- **Instagram Reels first** (OpusClip account StoryVaults 69ee71f1da7dbfe118f6e80e / sub 17841429829712746),
  then TikTok, then YouTube.
- **YouTube test:** also post one standalone 10–20 s gag clip (cut from new shots, 0 credits) per day.
- **Kill rule:** if the next 3 YouTube uploads each have < 100 views at 48 h, YouTube moves to a fresh
  channel whose seed audience is cat/animation viewers (Nolan's decision; it costs the 943 subs).
