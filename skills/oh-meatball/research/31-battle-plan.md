# 31 — Battle plan (Mayor, 2026-10-24)

This plan is built from research/26 (math), 27 (case studies), 28 (Shorts virality), 29 (funnel) and 30 (red team).

## The goal, restated with the facts
- **Hard deadline: 1 Feb 2027.** After that, new applicants need 1,000 subscribers plus 8,000 watch hours, or 20M Shorts views
  in 90 days (YouTube Help 12843009). Channels already in YPP are unaffected.
- **Target: meet today's rules by about 1 Jan 2027** (1,000 subscribers + 4,000 watch hours, or + 10M Shorts views in 90 days),
  so that the roughly month-long review finishes before February.
- **Fallback: the lower tier** (500 subscribers + 3,000 hours or 3M Shorts views) as soon as we qualify.
- "Monetized in 30 days" is not achievable (under 1%). The 30-day job is to **produce the breakout and pass review cleanly**.

## What the evidence says wins (and what doesn't)
1. **One hit does it.** Every credible fast case had a single long-form hit that supplied most of the watch hours
   (research/27). Small videos never add up to 4,000 hours.
2. **Shorts bring subscribers, long-form brings hours.** Shorts-feed time doesn't count toward watch hours.
3. **The first second decides a Short.** Winners open on the surprising claim, with the animal named in the first
   2 words. None of the 43 top animal Shorts opens with "TRUE or FALSE" (research/28).
4. **Danger-to-you and gross biology win; cute trivia loses.** Our own data agrees (research/28).
5. **Templated volume gets channels rejected.** Four channels reached the numbers and were refused for reused or
   repetitive content (research/27, research/30).
6. **Posting volume alone doesn't work:** 300 Shorts produced 131k views (research/27).
