# 90 · Why Wednesday's Shorts fell (read-only, 11/12 09:45 ET)
**Answer:** Not a fixed daily pool and not creature choice: every Short gets a first batch of about 1,000 views on day 0 (90% of its life) and Wednesday's four never got it. Whether YouTube showed them to few people or viewers swiped away cannot be told without one Studio screenshot (Q5).
Sources: Windsor YouTube 34639 (last data day **11/9**; Shorts split by `creator_content_type`; day assumed Pacific), yt-dlp flat listing 11/12 09:39 ET (public views, rounded above 1K), research 69/75/89, web pages named below. Rows: `data/90-youtube-daily.csv`, `data/90-youtube-video-day.csv`.

## Most likely causes, ranked
1. Weak first-audience response on specific Shorts. For: 3 of 5 earlier lows under the 0.39 engaged floor; day-0 gate at about 1,000 so a bad first batch ends the Short; Wed Shorts barely moved after day 0. Against: no 11/10+ retention data; roach #53 has a proven creature; two of five lows were inside the winners' range.
2. Too many near-identical bug Shorts to the same viewers within 9 hours (5 on 11/11, falling by order). For: the order decay on 11/11 only. Against: Sherman 2023 says no frequency effect; 10/25 and 10/27 had six each at about 1K; total fell below three-upload days; no primary source either way.
3. Topic or creature. For: lady beetle, boxelder, camel cricket, termite never tried. Against: roach #53, wolf spider 1.0K, peak search demand for two flops. Weakest.
