# 53 · Channel SWOT, 2026-10-27

Data: vidIQ channel stats (10/27), Metricool YouTube "published in range" and TikTok post analytics 10/15-10/27
(YouTube Analytics lags 2-3 days, so 10/26-10/27 Shorts are missing).

## Numbers
- YouTube: 959 subs (+15 since 10/22). 15.3k public views. Shorts average about 900 views, best 1,904 (daddy longlegs).
  Shorts AVD 4-15 s. Long-form 4-49 views each, AVD 34-37 s. Total watch time since 10/22 is about 31 hours.
- Subscriber conversion is about 0.1% (15 subs per ~15k views).
- Comments: 4 across 20 YouTube uploads. Shares: 1. Likes about 1% of views.
- TikTok: 9,363 followers. Typical post 60-700 views. Outlier: bombardier beetle (63 s, photo style, "would you pick
  one up?", toads throw it up alive) 5,767 views, 88 likes, 3 shares, and +44 followers that day. Nothing else
  above 800. Drawn and story posts from 10/26-27 have no data yet.

## Strengths
- Production is fast and cheap: one outline gives both cuts in ~15 min with no paid tools.
- Shorts get a steady ~1k views each, so the algorithm tests every Short.
- Series titles and scary-myth questions are the top YouTube group (daddy longlegs, Would Meatball Survive, Meatball vs Spiders).
- TikTok has a real audience base (9.4k) and Creator Rewards.

## Weaknesses
- Long-form gets almost no impressions. 4,000 watch hours is out of reach on the current path.
- Retention: Shorts hold 4-15 s. Nobody finishes, so YouTube doesn't widen distribution.
- Engagement is near zero. Written comment prompts in descriptions don't work.
- Sub conversion is 0.1%; views don't turn into a reason to come back.
- Too many formats at once (quiz, case, story, drawn, photo). The audience can't tell what the channel is.
- 4 off-brand "Oh Meatball" wedding-drama videos are still public and confuse the topic signal.
- Queue thins after 11/4 (1-2 posts/day), and many queued TikToks are under 60 s, so they earn nothing.

## Opportunities
- Gross or absurd defence facts plus a physical-choice question ("would you pick one up?") are the only proven TikTok
  outlier. Make more of exactly that.
- Numbered series raise Shorts. "Things Living In Your House" can run 10+ parts.
- Compilations: stitching a series' TikTok cuts into an 8-10 min long-form costs nothing and is the only cheap source
  of watch hours.
- 41 subs to 1,000. That unlocks nothing alone but is a psychological milestone for Nolan.

## Threats
- Shorts path to YPP needs 10M views in 90 days (~110k/day). Today it's ~6k/day, an 18x gap. Only outliers close it.
- Posting 9 TikToks a day can split the audience and drag the account average. TikTok judges each post on early
  retention.
- AI-content labelling and reused-content rules: keep every video original, with the AI label on.
- vidIQ credits are down to ~68 until 11/24.

## Fixes (this round)
1. Stop making new long-forms. Already-rendered ones stay scheduled. Long-form = compilation of finished series.
2. Every TikTok 61 s+: replace sub-60 queued TikToks with drawn cuts, nearest date first.
3. Double down on the outlier shape: series "Would You Pick It Up?" (gross/absurd defences, drawn, 61 s+), alongside
   "Things Living In Your House".
4. Fill the thin queue after 11/4 with those two series only, max 4 TikToks and 3 Shorts a day.
5. Spoken engagement: the comment question is said in the video, not just written in the caption.
6. Nolan: unlist the 4 "Oh Meatball" wedding-drama videos in YouTube Studio (free, 1 min; costs 20 vidIQ credits via API).
