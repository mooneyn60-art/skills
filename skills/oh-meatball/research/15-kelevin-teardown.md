## Format
- Visuals: flat cartoon illustrations, thick outlines, recurring stick-figure mascot in a gold crown/hat. Stylized on purpose, so "AI-looking" is a non-issue.
- Thumbnails: mascot + 1-2 illustrated subjects + 2-3 huge words ("complete lies", "Last Stand", "Worse Than You Think"), white/flat background.
- Script: list of 8-12 myths, ~1-1.5 min each. Pattern per item: state the myth -> "that's wrong" -> the funnier real story -> punchline. Joke every 2-4 sentences, roasting the viewer ("You fool. You buffoon."), running gags ("Alex"), callbacks, dry asides ("still charging too much").
- Opens on a visual cold-open joke within 5 s, no intro. Ends: "if you like this video you'll like this one" + subscribe.
- Titles: "[Topic] 'Facts' Everyone Still Believes", "... That Turned Out To Be True", "... That Sound Fake But Are Real", "How Every X Died", "The Worst X From Every Time Period".

## Why it matters for us
- Long-form = fastest route to YPP (4,000 watch hours): one 12-min video averaging ~50% retention needs ~40k views for 4k hours.
- Low volume (2/month) — quality over quantity, fits our budget.
- Stylized art removes the "obviously AI" problem entirely.
