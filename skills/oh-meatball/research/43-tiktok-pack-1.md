## Rules applied (OPERATIONS.md)
- The animal is named in the first 2 words, and frame 0 shows the photo and the claim.
- A new picture comes every 3 s or less.
- One Fork + tink per video.
- No TRUE/FALSE card, and no ask in the first 3 s.
- 11 of 21 end on a spoken "…? Comment." just before the loop line.
- Voice is Bill.
- Every fact is web-checked, with sources on each hook beat. Uncertain numbers are softened ("about", "up to", "may", "likely").
