# 50 · Outlier gaps and trend-setting ideas (Intel desk, 2026-10-26)

**Brief (Nolan, 10/26).** "Is there anything our outliers are doing that's working that we're not, or something we can
do to pave the way and set the trend?"
**Answer in one line.** The winners in our new niche are **long Shorts (median 1:16, and 17 of 25 run a minute or more, not 20 s), told by a visible
presenter, and they ask viewers for *their* theories**. We do none of the three. Nobody in the niche gives a verdict, so
the trend we can set is **the honest investigator who rules on the week's viral claim, with viewers' cases on the docket**.

`vidiq_trending_videos` Shorts 5, three transcripts 15 (Voggle *Mike the Headless Chicken*, Mister Magic *Chess Robot*,
HistoryFactsUsa *People That Science Still Cannot Explain*). No `video_watch` (25 credits would have been 60% of the
budget; the IG/TikTok search already returns per-video hook, format and pacing breakdowns).

### Top 5 gaps, ranked by expected impact [I]
1. **Length.** 68% of the Shorts outliers run 60 s or more (median 1:16); ours are 20-25 s. We already make 61-75 s
   deep dives for TikTok (research/40 open-loop gate), so port them to YouTube Shorts as a test.
2. **A visible presenter.** 8 of the 16 IG/TikTok outliers show a person (split screen or talking head, 22-89x their
   medians). Host shots fill this gap (research/49). The split-screen layout is the next step.
3. **Asking for their theory.** We get about 0.2 comments per 1k views; the question-led winners get 2.7-3.8 per 1k.
4. **Long-form length and lists.** 17 of 20 long-form outliers run 25 min or more, and 8 of 20 are lists or
   compilations. Ours run 5-7 min, one case each.
5. **Part 2 and series.** Cliffhangers and "full episode" hand-offs are standard; we have none.

## 5. Caveats
- Outlier multipliers are relative to each channel's own median; several channels are tiny (84-9.8K subs), so a 2,944x
  outlier means "far above its usual", not "huge".
- Lengths come from vidIQ durations; "presenter on screen" comes from vidIQ's per-video format labels for IG/TikTok,
  not from my own viewing.
- Comment rates for the trending Shorts include big channels (Dustin Hadley 1.31M subs, Game Changer 2.53M), so
  2.7-3.8 per 1k is the target shape, not a promise for a 1K-sub channel.
