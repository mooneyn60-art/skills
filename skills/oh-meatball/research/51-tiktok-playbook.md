## 2. TikTok Playlists by series (free, on the profile grid)
TikTok **Playlists** group our posts into tabs on the profile. Don't confuse them with TikTok **Series**, which is
the *paid* collections product. Never use Series. Playlists need eligible accounts; it shows as "Create playlist"
under the profile's video tab [UNV: eligibility varies by region and account].

| Playlist (exact name) | What goes in | Order |
|---|---|---|
| `🔍 Meatball Investigates: Cases` | every numbered case (Case #N) and its deep cut | by case number |
| `🕳️ Verdict on the Viral One` | the Wednesday verdicts | #1 first |
| `📂 Viewer Case Friday` | the Friday viewer cases | oldest first |
| `🦈 Meatball vs Animals` | the animal posts (Would Meatball Survive?, vs Spiders, animal jeopardy) | newest first |

A playlist turns one good video into a binge. The viewer who finishes Case #12 sees "Cases (14)" one tap away.

## 3. TikTok SEO: be findable in TikTok search
TikTok search is now a real traffic source for "explained" content. Our posts get 0% search traffic so far
(Metricool's source split is empty for the 10/23-10/26 posts).
- **Caption line 1 = the searched phrase**, in plain words: "Is the Bermuda Triangle real?", "What was MKUltra?",
  "Mary Celeste ghost ship explained". The one-tap question comes second. That reverses today's order for case posts
  only. Animal posts keep the question first, since they're feed-led. The postmaster applies it to new case posts.
- **On-screen text:** the 3-5 word `headline` on frame 0 already holds the topic word ("MIND CONTROL?"). Keep the
  proper noun in it ("MKULTRA: MIND CONTROL?"). TikTok reads on-screen text and the auto-captions.
- **Spoken keyword in the first 3 seconds.** Say the name of the case ("Operation Northwoods") in line 1. The v2 case
  shape mostly does this already.
- **Hashtags:** 3-5. One broad (`#history` or `#conspiracy`), one topic (`#bermudatriangle`), and `#meatballinvestigates`.
  No `#fyp` spam. Never use file-nickname tags (OPERATIONS rule).
- **Turn on auto-captions** at post time (TikTok indexes them). Our burned-in captions are for viewers; the platform
  captions are for search.

## 4. Trending sounds, used legally and quietly
- **The rule:** only sounds from TikTok's own library, added in the app, under our voice, at low volume (TikTok's
  "Volume" slider: original sound 100%, added sound 5-10%). The music bed we render in stays. The added sound is
  there only for the sound page and the trend signal.
- **Which library:** a **personal** account can use the general library. A **business** account can only use the
  **Commercial Music Library** (CML). Trending pop songs are often not in the CML [3P]. So account type (above) also
  decides this.
- **The blocker:** we post through Metricool's API, and the API can't attach a library sound. So this lever is
  in-app only. **One test (7 days):** Metricool sends the daily 18:00 animal repost to Nolan's TikTok inbox as a draft
  (if Metricool offers "send as draft / notification" for TikTok [UNV]). Nolan opens it, taps Sounds, picks a trending
  sound from the top of the list at 5-10%, and posts. That's 1 minute a day. **Score it:** views at 24 h against the same slot's
  median the week before. Keep it only at 1.3x or more, since it costs Nolan time every day.
- `tiktok_music_trending` (Higgsfield) returned 0 tracks on 10/24 (research/35 §3). Don't rely on it.

## 5. Stitch the viral claim, for "Verdict on the Viral One" (Nolan, in-app)
The Wednesday verdict exists because the viral videos never give one (research/50). **Stitch** puts up to 5 s of
the original claim in front of our answer, natively, with credit and a link back to the creator. The creator's
audience gets shown the reply.
- **How (Nolan, 2 min):** on the viral video → **Share** → **Stitch** → trim to the claim (≤5 s) → **Next** → upload our
  rendered verdict from the phone's camera roll (the `tt-*` deep cut, or the Short) → caption → post.
- **Only stitch a video that has Stitch turned on** (the button is greyed out otherwise). Never download and re-upload
  someone's clip. That's the reused-content line (OPERATIONS "Never show other creators' clips").
- **Respect:** we rule on the *claim*, never mock the *creator*. No living private people, nothing on the red list,
  and no stitches of minors' accounts.
- **Money:** Stitches earn **$0 Creator Rewards** (research/32). So a stitch is an **extra** post on top of the 4 a day,
  never in place of a 60 s+ Rewards slot. The normal un-stitched verdict deep cut still posts at 19:30.
- **Pick (Tuesday's marketing desk, with the verdict pick):** TikTok search for the claim → filter "This week" →
  the most-viewed video with Stitch enabled and no verdict in its comments. Put the link in the verdict script's `_note`.
- **Score:** stitch views at 48 h against the un-stitched verdict deep cut's views, plus profile visits (Metricool TKEV09).

## 6. Pins, and Nolan's Promote campaign (E-promote)
Nolan paid for **TikTok Promote with a follower goal** on 10/26. The promoted post is the **Bombardier Beetle deep dive** (63 s,
posted 10/26 09:30). This is his spend. We add none. Budget, duration and audience are still to come from him (data/experiments.csv
E-promote).

**Getting the most from Promote:**
1. **Promote the best case video, not the newest post.** A follow-goal ad works when the video tells a new viewer what
   they'd be following. The best candidate has (a) a strong first second: the claim and the proper noun on frame 0, (b) the
   series identity on screen: the MEATBALL INVESTIGATES tag, the CASE # card, the verdict stamp, and (c) above-median
   completion. The beetle is a good animal video, but it isn't the show we're building. New followers from it expect
   animals and may skip cases.
2. **Next time, promote** the case TikTok with the highest *full-watch rate* among posts that are 48 h+ old and have
   300+ views. Read it in TikTok Studio → Analytics → Content, or Metricool `TKPO13`. Pick only from the 60 s+ deep cuts
   (`tt-*`), so the promoted viewers also see a Rewards-eligible format. Until there's 48 h data (first read Wed 10/28),
   the **default pick is Case #8, "The CIA Stole a Soviet Submarine" (`tt-glomar`, 10/28 20:00)**. A PROVEN Cold War case
   with a strong scene-first hook, our strongest sub-format by search and outlier data (research/48 §3).
3. **Keep posting fresh cases daily while it runs.** Promote sends people to the profile. The profile has to show a new
   case from today, the pins and the Cases playlist.
4. **Pin the 3 best case videos** on the profile (video → **⋯** → **Pin**). Until 48 h data exists, pin the first three case
   posts: Nuke the Moon (A119), Roswell, Northwoods. On Thu 10/29, swap in the 3 case posts with the best full-watch
   rate. Re-check every Monday.
5. **Pause rule:** stop the campaign when **cost per follower** (budget spent ÷ followers gained, both in the Promote
   dashboard) runs over the cap Nolan sets. Our suggestion: **$0.10 per follower**. That's an assumption, not a measured
   benchmark, so Nolan should replace it with his own number. Also stop after 3 days if the promoted followers' week-after
   views per post (below) don't move.
6. **How we'll judge it (Intel, weekly):**
   - followers gained and cost per follower;
   - follower quality: median views per post and full-watch rate of posts in the 7 days after the campaign vs the 7 days
     before (pre-campaign median ~200 views/post);
   - profile views (`TKEV09`);
   - the Creator Rewards gap: followers to 10K, and last-30-day views to 100K (TikTok Studio → Analytics → Overview,
     "Last 30 days").
   If followers cross 10K but views per post don't rise, the money bought a number, not an audience. Say so.
