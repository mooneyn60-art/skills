# Channel knowledge (seed)

Tested findings with numbers, one line each. Copy to skills/oh-meatball/KNOWLEDGE.md and keep it growing.
Seeded by TARS on 2026-10-24 from research/24 and research/42.

- **82% of views come from the Shorts feed** (4,603 of ~5,600, Oct 1-24). Shorts are the engine; long-form is not yet.
- **~60% swipe away in the first second** (1,820 engaged of 4,603). The people who stay watch 128% (they finish and rewatch). The opening is the problem, not the content.
- **Search is source #2** (886 views at 64% viewed, all Shorts). Titles phrased as the question people type win there.
- **Long-form: 5 views in 30 days, ~28 s watched.** Zero suggested traffic. Daily 11-minute episodes are not paying off yet.
- **0 subscribers from ~5,600 views.** Nothing recurring gives a reason to subscribe.
- **Short length:** best Shorts ran 27-49 s (river shark 769, goldfish 661); the 88 s lemming got 188. Outlier rivals run 17-30 s.
- **Character-led "weird true things" channels break out** (Mister Fabio 288x, Sam O'Nella compilations 1,618x); topic-led quizzes don't.
