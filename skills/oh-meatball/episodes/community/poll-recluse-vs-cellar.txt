A = brown recluse, B = cellar spider, basement).

POST:
Only one of these can send you to the hospital 🏥🕷️ Which one is in YOUR basement?
Options: A / B

ANSWER POST (2 days later):
It's A, the brown recluse 🕷️ Its bite can cause a serious skin wound in some people, so if you think one bit you,
see a doctor (and bring the spider if you can). Check the eyes: a recluse has 6 eyes in 3 pairs; most spiders have 8.
B is a cellar spider, the "daddy longlegs" in your basement corner. Its fangs usually can't even pierce your skin.
Good news: one Kansas home had 2,055 brown recluses, and nobody was bitten. And recluses mostly live in the south-central
and midwestern US, so many "recluse bites" turn out to be something else.
