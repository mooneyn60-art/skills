Your life as a house cat, from a newborn kitten with its eyes still shut to an old cat: eight ways it goes wrong, what you get back, and whether it's a raw deal or a sweet one.

Eight things come with being a house cat, in the order they hit you: a deal made thousands of years ago, a five-week window to learn humans, senses built for another job, a body that needs taurine, a house full of lilies, pills, string and windows, the carrier, your human, and your own ageing body. Then we look at the other deal outside the door, and Meatball reads the ledger.

House cat facts, life as a house cat, why cats most likely can't taste sugar, lilies and cats, cats and human pain pills, cats falling from windows, the cat carrier and the vet, neutering, declawing, the cry inside a cat's purr, kidney disease in older cats, joint disease on X-rays, indoor cats and the risks outside.
