Before your Christmas tree comes through the door, check it for one thing: a tan foamy blob on a twig that can look like a toasted marshmallow. Inside it: a hundred eggs, maybe several hundred, and your warm living room is about to tell them it's spring. Nine things can ride in on a real Christmas tree. Six you can ignore, one you vacuum, one you clip off, and one matters outside your house. Meatball checks the "25,000 bugs" number first, then walks the whole tree check with a PANIC METER on screen the whole way.

THE TREE CHECK
1. Look for the marshmallow
2. Clip it, put it in an evergreen
3. Shake it, then move it away
4. Pull any bird nest, check the trunk
5. Vacuum. Never squash. Never spray.
