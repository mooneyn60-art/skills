## Webbing clothes moth
**Files:** `yt-clothesmoth-explainer.mp4` (15.8 s) · `yt-clothesmoth-loop.mp4` (7.2 s)
**Title:** The Moth Isn't Eating Your Sweater 🧶 (Things Living In Your House)
**Loop title:** The Moth Can't Eat Your Sweater 🧶 (Things Living In Your House)
**Description:** Adult clothes moths don't feed at all. The holes come from their larvae: a female lays about 40-50 eggs on wool, and the babies eat it. Sweaty, stained wool gets eaten more, and a few days in the freezer (bagged, below 18°F) kills them. 🧶

Sources: Univ. of Kentucky Entomology, ENTFACT-609 Clothes Moths; UC IPM Pest Notes: Clothes Moths (Pub. 7435).
