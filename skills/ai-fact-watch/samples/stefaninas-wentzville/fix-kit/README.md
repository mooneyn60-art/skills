# Fix Kit

Do these in order:

1. Paste `localbusiness.jsonld` into your website's <head> (inside a <script type="application/ld+json"> tag).
2. Publish `official-facts.html` at /official-facts and link it in your footer.
3. Upload `llms.txt` to your site root.
4. Make the edits in `gbp-edits.md`.
5. Correct the listings in `directories.md`.
6. Submit the feedback in `ai-feedback.md`.

We monitor and document; no one can guarantee an AI vendor will change its answer.
