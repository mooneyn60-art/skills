# Printable demand research — first 10 products

Researched 2026-10-15. Goal: digital downloads Etsy delivers automatically, in niches where
search demand is high relative to the number of competing listings.

## Method and data quality

- Etsy search pages refuse automated fetches (HTTP 403), so live result counts and bestseller
  badges could not be read directly. **Before relying on a niche, the owner should open the Etsy
  search once and confirm the listing count and the "Bestseller" badges on page 1.**
- Demand and competition figures come from RankHero's Etsy keyword pages. Its numbers are
  third-party estimates covering Sep 2025 to Sep 2026. Several pages show two different listing
  counts; both are recorded below. Treat the numbers as relative signals, not exact counts.
- **Ratio** = average monthly searches ÷ competing listings. A higher ratio means more buyers per
  competing listing. Seasonal keywords also show their peak month, because that is when the
  listing has to be live and indexed (2-4 weeks before the peak).
- Fees and pricing context: listing fee $0.20 per listing (renews every 4 months or on sale),
  6.5% transaction fee, and 3% + $0.25 payment processing in the US
  ([Gelato](https://www.gelato.com/blog/etsy-fees),
  [Marmalead](https://blog.marmalead.com/etsy-fees-explained/),
  [Checkoutpage](https://checkoutpage.com/blog/etsy-fees)).
- Niche strategy: generic terms ("weekly planner printable") are saturated. Narrow, specific
  variants convert better ([ListifyAI](https://www.listifyai.net/blog/low-competition-etsy-niches-digital-products),
  [InsightAgent](https://www.insightagent.app/guides/etsy-printables-ideas)). Kids' activities,
  holiday printables, and planners show up repeatedly as steady printable categories
  ([Unilink](https://www.unilink.us/blog/best-things-to-sell-on-etsy-2026),
  [PromptlessPress](https://www.promptlesspress.com/blog-best-selling-digital-products-etsy-2026)).

Other observations:

- Large-print word search listings target seniors, include answer keys, and sell at $2.99 to $3.99
  for 75 to 100 puzzles, with a few large bundles near $8
  ([example listing](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4452104530),
  [RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/large-print-word-search)). Tags that sellers use include
  "senior activities", "brain games seniors", "dementia activities", and "gift for grandma".
- Thanksgiving game listings are instant PDF downloads in 8.5x11 plus smaller sizes, priced from about
  $1.27 to $6. Common formats are bingo, trivia, prize-passing rhymes, and icebreakers
  ([listing](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1580188826), [listing](https://www.etsy.com/ca/listing/1806447279)).
- Wedding and baby shower games have large demand but low ratios (0.38 to 0.42). They are a later
  expansion once the shop has reviews.
- Avoid formats built on trademarked games (for example, family game-show and category-game names).
  Many listings use those names and carry takedown risk. Bingo, trivia, word search, and
  "would you rather" are generic formats.

## Timing (today is 2026-10-15)

- Thanksgiving is 2026-11-26. Its keywords climb in October and peak in November, so Thanksgiving
  listings must go live now.
- Christmas keywords are about 10x higher in November than in October, and peak in December.
  Christmas listings should go live by early November so they are indexed before the climb.
- Classroom jobs peak in August. An October listing builds history before January resets and the
  next back-to-school season.
