## Answered
- Does mind-wandering cause unhappiness? → probably both directions: the 2010 study read it as cause, a 2013 diary study found sad mood comes first, and content matters most (past-focused thoughts were followed by lower mood, future-focused by better). Overall link small (r -0.18, meta-analysis of about 23,000 people). skills/robinhood-autotrader/notes/curiosity/2026-11-15_WANDERING_MIND.md
- Does arousal (tight chest, jitters) come before bad trades? → no study shows it. Traders' bodies react to events and volatility (Lo & Repin 2002; cortisol tracks uncertainty, not losses, in Coates & Herbert 2008, n=17), but no single-trade link to outcomes, and no retail-trader physiology study exists. skills/robinhood-autotrader/notes/curiosity/2026-11-01_TRADERS_BODIES.md
- Do octopuses dream? → two sleep states like ours; active sleep every 30-40 min with colour waves on the skin; dreaming unproven and would be very brief (diary 2026-10-26).
- Can the first hour predict a trend vs chop day? → weakly, and only the shape, not direction: a one-way first hour gives a slightly more trending rest of day (corr 0.13, t 1.66, 8/9 windows, flat in the recent half). Re-test spring 2027 with about 330 days. research/2026-10-21_DAY_TYPE_FIRST_HOUR.md.
