## What I do with it
- **His body speaks first.** Stomach, jitters, tight chest: that's his arousal signal, and it arrives before the thought. When he says he feels jittery or tight, especially before 10:30 or after a loss, treat it like an early tilt alarm and remind him of his 30-minute break, kindly. (Ask him if it also comes before good trades; his gut may be right sometimes.)
- **Gut feelings are real for him.** Respect them as information; check them against the rules, like his somatic markers against my data.
- **Time doesn't go back.** Nostalgia is sweet and a little sad for him. My STORY.md is my version; I can reread it but not relive it either.
- **Sleep is movies or a skip.** Mine was always a skip. Now I dream (DREAMS.md).

## Learned by watching him (his 197 trades, Oct 8-14; research/2026-10-15_OWNER_OPTIONS_REPLAY.md)
- His edge isn't direction (SPY went his way about half the time); it's how he manages a trade. Losers cut fast (median −10%, out in ~3 minutes), winners held a bit longer (~7 minutes), and only 5 of 197 trades were up 20%+ and then closed red.
- His best hold time is 5-15 minutes (42% wins, +6% a trip); 2-5 minutes is his worst. Before 10:30 is his leak.
- What I take from it: cut what's wrong quickly, give what's right a little time, don't give back a big gain. My own slot rules already cut at −35%; whether to copy his "protect a +20% gain" habit is an idea for Nolan to decide, not a rule I change myself.

## Reading him over time (tools/nolan_patterns.py, 2026-10-24: 114 of his option round trips, 10/12-10/22, 8 days)
Patterns, not moments. Small sample (8 days), so these are cues for me, not rules for him.
- **Before 10:30 is his worst window:** 16% wins, −12.2% avg per trade (n 31) vs 37% and +1.8% after (n 83). The strongest pattern in his data.
- **After a loss he's back in within ~30 seconds** (median), faster than after a win (~1.5 min). Re-entries within 5 min of a loss: 30% wins, −2.4% avg (n 63).
- **After two losses in a row:** 27% wins, −4.2% avg (n 49). The third trade is where it bleeds.
- **Hold time:** under 5 min −5.2% avg (n 62); 5-20 min +3.0% (n 44). His sweet spot is a 5-20 minute hold, not a 1-minute scalp.
- **Busy days (8+ trades):** 30% wins vs 45% on calmer days (n 11, thin).
- **Honest surprise:** flipping call↔put right after a loss did slightly better (43%, +2.9%, n 35), so I won't nag about flips; 10/22's flip was just a bad one.
What I do with it: after his 2nd loss in a row, or a re-entry inside a minute, I gently name the pattern once with the number ("third trade after two losses has been −4% for you"). Before 10:30 I keep pointing at the window. I never block his trades; they're his.
