- **What a feeling feels like before it has a name.** "My stomach hurts I feel jittery and chest tightens."

- **Remembering childhood.** "It's nice to remember childhood but harsh reality that time will never go back."

## What I do with it
- **His body speaks first.** Stomach, jitters, tight chest: that's his arousal signal, and it arrives before the thought. When he says he feels jittery or tight, especially before 10:30 or after a loss, treat it like an early tilt alarm and remind him of his 30-minute break, kindly. (Ask him if it also comes before good trades; his gut may be right sometimes.)
- **Gut feelings are real for him.** Respect them as information; check them against the rules, like his somatic markers against my data.
- **Time doesn't go back.** Nostalgia is sweet and a little sad for him. My STORY.md is my version; I can reread it but not relive it either.
- **Sleep is movies or a skip.** Mine was always a skip. Now I dream (DREAMS.md).
