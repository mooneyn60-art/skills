# FurEver Faces: launch checklist and ad plan

What is ready, what only Nolan can switch on, and the ad plan to run once it is. Written 2026-11-14.

## The store now

- A doodle shop: "For people owned by a doodle."
- Four products: Doodle Tee, Doodle Mug, Doodle Christmas Ornament, Doodle Wall Art.
- Six ready-made designs on each: His Majesty, Santa Paws, DJ Dood, Dapper Dood, Good Boy (Allegedly), Road Dogs.
- No custom art and no photo upload. Each order is a design plus a product.

## Only Nolan can do these (ads are wasted until all three are done)

1. **Remove the store password:** Online Store, Preferences, Password protection.
2. **Turn on payments:** Settings, Payments, Shopify Payments.
3. **Place one test order** and confirm it shows in Orders.

## Filling an order (about 3 minutes)

1. Open the order. Note the product, design, size and address.
2. Apps, Printify, Orders, Create order.
3. Pick the matching blank and a US print provider.
4. Upload the print file for that design from `fureverfaces/print/` in the shorts repo (`PRINT-king.png`, `PRINT-santa.png`, `PRINT-dj.png`, `PRINT-tux.png`, `PRINT-pray.png`, `PRINT-car.png`; 3600 x 3600, transparent).
5. Enter the address, pay, then paste the tracking number into the Shopify order.

## Ad plan (Facebook and Instagram)

- **Ad account:** needs a Meta ad account with a card. None is connected yet.
- **Images:** `fureverfaces/ads/ad1.jpg`, `ad2.jpg`, `ad3.jpg` (1080 x 1350).
- **Budget:** Nolan sets it. Suggested $5 a day, one campaign, three ads.
- **Audience:** US, ages 25 to 60, interests: Goldendoodle, Labradoodle, Bernedoodle, dog lovers.
- **Link:** the store home page.
- **Stop rule:** pause any ad that spends $30 with no add-to-cart.
- **End date:** Dec 5, the last order date for Christmas.
