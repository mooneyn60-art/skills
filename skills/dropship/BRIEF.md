# FurEver Faces: launch brief

The plan for the agent building Nolan's custom pet-portrait store, from a live check of the store on 11/14 and what works in this niche. Written by TARS for Nolan.

Last Updated: 2026-11-14
Status: brief for the builder agent
Audience: Nolan's store agent, Nolan

## Overview
**What the store is:**
- FurEver Faces (Shopify Basic, USD, US)
- personalised pet-portrait gifts made to order (print on demand)
- 10 active products: ornaments, mugs, socks, a tee, canvas, a bandana, a sherpa blanket, a memorial ornament
- built 11/14; 9 sessions and 0 orders in the last 30 days (it just opened)

**The window:** this is a Christmas store, and the selling season is about 4 weeks. Personalised items need production (2-5 days) plus shipping (3-8 days), so **the last safe US order date is around Dec 8-12**, depending on the supplier. Everything below is ordered to make sales before then.

## Stop-ship problems found on 11/14 (fix before ANY traffic)
1. **No product has a single image.** Every product shows `featuredMedia: null`. Nobody buys a personalised gift they can't see. Each product needs:
   - 4-6 images: a mockup of the product with a real pet portrait on it, a before/after (pet photo → portrait), a lifestyle shot (mug on a desk, ornament on a tree), and a size/scale shot
   - one short video if possible
2. **Customers need a way to upload their pet's photo.** Check that every product page has a working upload field plus a pet-name field, and that the photo actually reaches the supplier with the order. Use a personalisation app tied to the print supplier (Printify, Printful, Gelato and Teeinblue all have Shopify personalisation), or line-item properties. **Place one real test order end to end** (upload → order → the supplier receives the photo → proof) before spending a dollar on traffic.
3. **Confirm the print supplier is connected** and every SKU (FF-ORN-RND, FF-MUG-11, …) maps to a supplier product. An order with no supplier attached is a refund and an angry customer.
4. **Delete the empty draft** "Untitled Nov 14 at 11:34 AM".
5. **Margins:** check each price against the supplier's cost plus shipping. The ornament ($21.99) and socks ($19.99) are likely thin once shipping is paid. Rule: the supplier cost plus shipping should be ≤ 45% of the price, so ads have room. The blanket ($94.99, tagged free shipping) needs its shipping cost confirmed.
6. **Policies and trust:**
   - refund policy (personalised = no change-of-mind returns; free reprint if damaged or wrong)
   - shipping policy with the Christmas cutoff date
   - contact page with a real email
   - privacy policy, an About page
   - Shopify Payments plus Shop Pay, Apple Pay and Google Pay turned on

## What makes these stores sell
- **Lead with the portrait, not the product.** People buy "my dog as a royal painting"; the mug is the delivery method. Hero section: a big before/after of a real pet, one line ("Your pet, as royalty. Ready for Christmas."), and a button.
- **A digital proof before printing** ("we'll email your portrait for approval") removes the #1 fear with custom gifts and cuts refunds.
- **A deadline everywhere:** a "Order by Dec 10 for Christmas delivery" bar sitewide, plus a countdown on product pages. True dates only.
- **Bundles raise order value:** mug + matching bandana, ornament 2-pack (one for grandma), "family pack" (tee + socks + bandana). Use a "frequently bought together" app or a simple bundle product.
- **Reviews:** install a reviews app. After the first orders, ask every customer for a photo review. Never fake reviews: the FTC fines for that, and buyers spot it.
- **The memorial ornament is the emotional product.** Give it gentle copy and its own collection ("In loving memory"). It often has the highest conversion in this niche, and it needs no Christmas joke.
- **Speed:** mobile-first theme, compressed images, few apps. Most traffic will be on phones.

## Traffic plan (in order; organic first, paid only once the store converts)
1. **TikTok and Instagram Reels, organic, daily.**
   - The proven format: "I turned my dog into a royal portrait and gave it to my mom", reaction videos, before/after reveals, "rating your pets' portraits".
   - Use Nolan's own pets or willing friends' pets, with permission.
   - Link in bio to the store.
   - TikTok Shop is an option later.
2. **Nolan's own audience:** Nolan already runs a pet/animal channel (Meatball Myths, TikTok monetized, about 1,000 YouTube subs, 18-34). A pinned comment or bio link to the store on pet videos is free traffic, but only on pet topics, never forced into every video, and only with Nolan's OK.
3. **Pinterest:** "pet lover gift ideas", "gifts for dog moms", "pet memorial gift" pins. Pinterest search peaks in November and December for gift ideas.
4. **Google Shopping (free listings)** through the Google & YouTube Shopping channel. Needs images, prices and the shipping policy in place first.
5. **Email/SMS:**
   - abandoned-cart flow (Shopify Email or Klaviyo free)
   - "finish your portrait" reminder
   - a welcome discount (10%) for signups
   - SMS needs opt-in consent
6. **Paid ads, only after organic proves the store converts** (at least 1% conversion on 100+ sessions, and a test order went through):
   - Meta or TikTok ads at a small fixed budget ($10-20/day)
   - 3 creatives
   - kill any ad with no add-to-cart after about $30
   - Nolan sets the budget; the agent never raises spend on its own

## Rules for the agent
- **No traffic or ads until images, the upload and a test order work.** Paying for traffic to a broken store burns money.
- **Never fake** reviews, scarcity ("only 2 left" on print-on-demand), "as seen on", or discounts from a made-up "original" price.
- **Never use** trademarked characters, team logos or brand names on products (Disney, NFL, Starbucks…): store takedowns and legal letters.
- **Customer pet photos are used only for their order,** never in ads without written permission.
- **Spending money** (apps over free tiers, ads, samples) needs Nolan's yes first.
- **Ask Nolan, don't guess:** which print supplier, whether he wants his own pets in videos, his ad budget, his profit target per order.
