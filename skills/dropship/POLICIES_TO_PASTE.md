## Refund policy
Every item is made to order with your pet's photo, so we can't accept returns or exchanges for change of mind, a wrong size chosen, or a photo you'd like to swap after printing.

If something isn't right, we'll make it right. If your item arrives damaged, misprinted, defective, or different from the proof you approved, email us within 30 days of delivery with your order number and a photo of the problem. We'll send a free replacement or a full refund, your choice. No need to send the item back.

Cancellations: you can cancel for a full refund until your item goes into production (usually within 24 hours of your order or of approving your proof).

Lost packages: if tracking shows no movement for 10 business days, contact us and we'll reship or refund.

## Shipping policy
We ship to the United States.

How long it takes: each item is printed to order. Production takes 2 to 5 business days, then shipping takes 3 to 8 business days. Most orders arrive in 1 to 2 weeks. You'll get a tracking link by email when your order ships.

Christmas delivery: to get your order before December 25, order by December 5. Orders placed after that date are made and shipped as fast as possible, but holiday delivery can't be guaranteed.

Shipping costs are shown at checkout before you pay.

Please double-check your shipping address at checkout. We can't redirect a package once it has shipped.
