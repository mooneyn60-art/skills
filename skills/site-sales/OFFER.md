# The offer: a simple website for your business

A one-page website that works well on phones, built from the details you
already share publicly, and reviewed with you before it goes live.

## What you get — $149, one time

- A one-page site: your name, what you sell or offer, hours or where to find
  you, and how to reach you.
- A clear contact or booking button (email, call, text, Instagram or a booking
  link you already use).
- A map link so people can find you.
- Built for phones first, fast to load, and readable on any screen.
- Up to two rounds of changes before launch, so every detail is correct.
- Hosting set up on a free static host, or on a domain you own. Domain
  registration (usually about $10–20 a year, paid by you directly to the
  registrar) is not included.
- You own the final files. If you stop working with us, you keep the site.

## Optional: $19 a month for updates

- Up to 30 minutes of text and photo changes a month (new hours, prices,
  seasonal items, closures).
- We keep the site online and check it still loads correctly.
- Cancel any time by replying to an email. No contract, no cancellation fee.

## What we don't promise

- We don't promise more customers, sales or search rankings. A website makes
  it easier for people who are already looking for you to find you and get in
  touch; how much that helps varies.
- We don't write reviews or testimonials, invent details, or use your photos
  without your permission.
- The preview you were sent is only a draft built from public listings. It is
  not published under your name and it is deleted if you're not interested.

## How it works

1. You look at the free preview.
2. If you'd like it, reply with any corrections and pay the one-time fee
   through the payment link in the email.
3. We make your changes, you approve, and the site goes live.
4. Add the monthly updates plan only if you want it.
