About

Poole Mill Produce is a second-generation hydroponic farm. Growing up, the owner helped her dad grow hydroponic tomatoes and cucumbers; in 2012 she and her husband Emanuel bought their own hydroponic operation. Everything is grown free of pesticides and herbicides and sold locally.

  • No pesticides or herbicides
  • Uses about 1/20 of the water of soil growing
  • Accepts Farmers' Market gift certificates and Market Bucks
