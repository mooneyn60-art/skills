About

Jim Lewis always wanted to be a farmer. On 1.5 acres of his wife's family land he grows all kinds of produce, starting plants in a hoophouse and using plant-based compost. He follows organic regulations but isn't certified. Jim has sold at the Bloomington Community Farmers' Market for 15 years, April through October.

  • All-natural growing, following organic rules (not certified)
  • Accepts Farmers' Market gift certificates and Market Bucks
  • At both the Saturday and Tuesday markets
