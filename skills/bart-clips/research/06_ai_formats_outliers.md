## Cross-format takeaways [I]

1. The top earners per video (ainimal-rescue-ai, KISHKA, StoryBurn, Cozy Paws) all have a **recognizable recurring cast or trope**. Pure templates (baby, ASMR) spike, then stall (30% stalled for baby).
2. Persona or voice beats template in history/POV: named hosts are growing 80–200% per month, while generic time-travel channels are flat.
3. Breakout speed is extreme across the board. Many channels created in Aug–Sep 2026 reached 100k–1M+ avg views within 3–8 weeks. Expect the window per format to be about 60–90 days before stall rates climb.
4. Enforcement noise (views −99%) is visible in rescue and baby niches. Disclosure and originality matter for staying monetized.
