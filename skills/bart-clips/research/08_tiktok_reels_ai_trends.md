# 08 — AI video trends on TikTok and Instagram Reels (Sept to Oct 2026)

Research date: 2026-10-15. Read-only; nothing was posted or published.

**Legend.** **[M]** = measured: a vidIQ data pull or a number from a cited source. **[I]** = my inference or judgment. **[R]** = reported by press or a blog and not independently verified.

## Method and data limits

- **vidIQ cross-platform outlier search.** 13 queries, filtered to posts after 2026-09-01. Most used a minimum of 500K views; the ASMR, vlog, security-cam, brainrot and street-interview queries used 300K. The "outlier" multiple is a post's views divided by that creator's median views. **[M]**
- **Account data.** I ran `vidiq_ig_accounts_from_outliers` on 3 niches and `vidiq_ig_profile` on 5 accounts. **[M]**
- **Web search.** Used for platform policy, tool share, and trend origins. Most of those sources are vendor blogs, so treat them with caution. **[R]**
- **vidIQ credits.** The balance went from 5,903 to 5,613, so this run used **290 credits**. The budget was 500.
- **Missing: account age.** No tool returns it. As a proxy I used post count against follower count, plus the dates of recent reels.
- **Missing: TikTok post dates.** The search returns no per-post date. I relied on the after-2026-09-01 filter instead.
- **Missing: Higgsfield music data.** `tiktok_music_trending` needs a `connector_id`, and that ID comes only from `tiktok_accounts`, which was outside the allowed read-only scope. So I pulled no Higgsfield music data.

## Headline findings

1. **The biggest winner is the AI animal or "anthro" soap-opera micro-drama with episodes.** This covers fruit, cat and pig-headed humans, with captions like "Part 2 / Ep 3". It produced more 1M to 13M view outliers across both platforms than any other format in this pull. **[M]**
2. **Hits are extremely concentrated.** A typical account's recent reels get 5K to 60K plays, with an occasional 5M to 30M hit. Examples: @darkpulseseuk had one reel at 12.9M while its others sat at 13K to 540K, and @itswofi had one reel at 30.6M while recent ones got 4K to 65K. **[M]** These accounts are lottery-ticket businesses that post daily. **[I]**
3. **Huge hits often bring few followers.** @idesignmedia.ai got 9.4M views on one POV-slide reel and has 8.5K followers. @reality_strokes got 4.3M and has 6.4K. @laibahamad_verse got 32.6M and has 10K. **[M]** Spectacle formats with no recurring character do not build an audience; formats with a recurring character do. **[I]**
4. **The audience bar is rising.** Instagram started limiting reach for undisclosed AI personas around 2026-08-31 **[R]**. TikTok has a "see less AI" slider, announced in November 2025 **[R]**. A Kapwing fresh-account test found 59% of TikTok For You videos were AI "slop", against roughly 20% on YouTube **[R]**.

- **Length and structure.** Instagram runs 60 to 90s. TikTok runs 90 to 500s, because of the over-60s Creator Rewards logic **[I]**. Episodic "Part 1/2/3" captions are standard. **[M]**

## Tools creators use

- **Seen in captions and bios [M].** Picsart (@botatoai), OpenArt (@deusofai), CapCut (@povmasterxz), Kling and Arcads (@deniz.akkabak, according to vidIQ's tag), Krea (@julesearns, in a "faceless channel" tutorial with 4.6M views).
- **Market data [R].** One 2026 tracker put Veo 3.1 at about 96% of measured video generations. Several sources say OpenAI discontinued the standalone Sora app on 2026-04-26 and shut the API down in September (unverified). Kling is described as the value and volume tool, with 60M+ creators by December 2025. Seedance 2.0 tops blind-preference leaderboards. Viggle and Kling power the baby and pet dance templates.
- **What creators likely use [I].** Fruit and cat dramas: Midjourney, Flux or Nano-Banana stills, then Kling or Hailuo image-to-video, plus ElevenLabs or CapCut TTS voices. Slideshow variants skip video generation entirely. Short-drama studios and POV slides: Veo 3.1, Kling 3 or Seedance, because of the native audio and physics. Higgsfield appears as a front-end for these models; no captions in this pull named it.

## Typical production specs (synthesis)

| Format | Length on Instagram | Length on TikTok | Audio | Hook |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fruit and cat drama | 60–90s | 90–500s | TTS dialogue and dramatic score | All-caps quote or "PART 2" in the first frame |
| Pet sitcom | 15–40s loop | 60–400s compilation | Sound effects, laugh track or trending track | Absurd visual in frame 1 (cat on phone in bed) |
| AI baby | 30–60s | 40–110s | Whimsical music and sound effects | Baby doing an adult task |
| POV slide or ASMR | 8–60s loop | Same | Raw sound effects, no voice | Drop or first cut |
| Historical POV | 60–100s | 60–100s | Ambient sound or narration | "POV: You wake up in [year]" |
| Short-drama | 30–160s | Same | Narration and score | Violence, child or animal in peril |

## Implications for bart-clips [I]

- **Use a recurring named character.** Accounts like @itswofi and @aistudiofruity convert views into followers; one-off spectacle accounts do not.
- **Cut long and short separately.** Post over-60s cuts on TikTok for Creator Rewards and 15 to 40s loops on Instagram.
- **Label AI content and avoid realistic fake humans or celebrities.** Instagram began penalizing undisclosed AI personas in August 2026.
- **Expect hit-driven distribution.** Plan for 1 in 20 to 1 in 50 posts doing more than 1M views.
