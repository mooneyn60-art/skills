## 4. Marketing

### Amazon (start once a book is live)
- **Amazon Ads:** open *Marketing → Amazon Ads* in KDP. For each book, create a
  Sponsored Products **automatic** campaign at **$5/day** with a **$0.40** default bid.
- **Break-even:** you make $2.55 a sale, so ad spend under about $2.55 per sale
  (28% ACoS) is profitable. Accept up to ~50% in the first month to build rank
  and reviews.
- **After 14 days:** move search terms that produced sales into a **manual exact**
  campaign. Add terms with 15+ clicks and no sales as negatives.
- **Author Central** (author.amazon.com): claim "Hollow Pine Puzzles", add a short
  bio, and link both books so they show as a series.
- **A+ Content** (KDP → Marketing): add a comparison chart of the series so each
  book advertises the other.

### Free channels
- **Pinterest** (the best free channel for quilting and knitting): make a free
  business account. Pin 3 times a week: sample puzzle pages, "can you find 10
  words?" images, and cover mockups, each linking to the Amazon page.
- **YouTube Shorts:** "Find 5 quilting words in 30 seconds" puzzle videos. I can
  make these automatically from the book files, and you have vidIQ connected for
  titles and tags.
- **Facebook groups:** join quilting and knitting groups and follow their rules.
  Share only in promo threads or where self-promotion is allowed. Being helpful
  first sells better than posting ads.
- **Local:** give one copy to a quilt guild or yarn club raffle. Ask local quilt
  and yarn shops about carrying copies (author copies cost about $3).
- **Reviews:** the thank-you page already asks politely. Never offer anything in
  exchange for a review (against Amazon policy).
